Description
Memento moribund.
A moribund patch of roadside desert, stretching far and wide: a one-way threshold of clumped cells, paused as though the ribcage had been reached betwixt its own ribs of motion and furiously squeezed still by the hand.
There are vehicles standing mid-movement; caught between lanes — these vehicles may unlock, should moribund permits — inside them are ordinary riches of interrupted life. Reside within them, and you will only hear the sound of your own hearing of your own.
This bloody Sunday afternoon, Felix lit his cigarette in a flash, thinking that maybe this will get better eventually. The lighter was green, with a silly cactus sticker on it that his friend had picked for him as a joke. He thought of this for a while, as the burning cigarette continuously took its small, patient toll.
He needed to get back to his friend. His friend didn't know whether Felix was still receiving his messages. Felix knew this; yet he did not know how he knew this. For what it is worth, Felix could not even remember the last one he had read.
Said cigarette burned down to his fingers before he noticed. He dropped it onto the asphalt below and watched the ember twitch there, starry bright against the bitumen-black. Felix was not going anywhere.
He rubbed his fingers together and looked down the highway. Nothing moved, just like the car that stopped in the opposite lane. Then another. Then another. Then another. Zero was the word that came out of Felix's mouth as he tried counting how many had their lights on.
The moribund highway stretched ahead of him, perfectly straight, the desert on either side pale beneath the afternoon sun. And in this moment, he wondered whether there was an end to it that would bring them together.
As Felix looked back he discovered the cigarette was gone. And so was his friend — but his friend had been out of his reach since long, long ago.
Felix coughed into his sleeve again; he had been coughing for some time. The fabric was damp with more blood as he pulled away. He looked at it for a moment, before looking back down at the other side of the road.
Felix was standing on the highway, waiting for the conqueror worm that his friend, cities away, prayed would never find him.
