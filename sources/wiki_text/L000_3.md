# Description
The description of this sub-section is nearly identical to Level 0. It's just that… there's ice. EVERYWHERE.
And it's so damn cold.

Additional: After that catastrophe in remodeling Level 0, we believe in you all that you shall bring a good set of clean, pristine, and comfortably warm rooms! So, you all better actually work with what we have provided you! Understood? Sincerely, Major Took
We are working hard to remodel Level 0.3 ! Summary We must say, these icy rooms are a mess, but rest assured, progress on fixing that is going smoothly! See those disgusting carpets laced with frost, coarse as gravel? We replaced them in the blink of an eye . See those bare walls sprinkled with scraps of palely frozen wallpapers, that can detach at a moment's notice? We covered them with our improved design. See those gigantic cubes of ice scattered throughout, within rooms so small in comparison that only barely contains them within their thin margins? These blowtorches excellently took care of them. Havec is doing some great work to pump out those gas for it!
What a mess… Who knew that walls can have frostbites too?
