Secure . Singular Nonhostile Presence . Level 263's entrance. Description: Level 263 , also known as Pretty Pink, is a seemingly endless, repetitive hallway with large, segmented windows on both sides. Both the walls and carpet are colored a vibrant shade of pink, with the carpet being slightly darker. The windows themselves do not function as actual windows, instead being solid surfaces with a constantly changing static texture. The ceiling is approximately 55 ft (~17 m) tall, and is pitch-black in color. Pink: Level 263 possesses a consciousness that can be spoken to through any device that can display text. This consciousness refers to herself as "Pink." Pink was able to access the GPD 1 and attempted several times to make an article about itself, asking (and later pleading) for wanderers to visit, under the account name "pretty_pink." Pink, when able to speak with wanderers, is incredibly friendly and eager to have a conversation. Likewise, she displays great sadness and disappointment when wanderers leave. Discovery As mentioned previously, Pink was only made known through her attempts to make GPD pages on herself. While these pages were removed, Researcher Blair Summerton reached out to the account and later visited the level. A recorded interaction with Level 263 and Pink is below. Entrances and Exits: Entrances Currently, the only known stable entrance to Level 263 is located near Base Beta in Level 11. However, entrances have sporadically been seen in numerous other levels, including (but not limited to) Level 0 , Level 5 , Level 37 , Level 141 , and Level 389 . Exits Level 263 can easily be exited by simply turning around.
