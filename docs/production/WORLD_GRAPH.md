# World graph

`world_graph.dot` (regenerated by `tools/build_reports.py`) has **exactly the 100 roster nodes**
plus one auxiliary node for destinations outside the roster. Render it with
`dot -Tsvg docs/production/world_graph.dot -o world_graph.svg`.

Legend: node fill shows implementation status (green = PLAYABLE, amber = IN_DEVELOPMENT,
grey = not built). Edge style shows source confidence: solid = CONFIRMED, dashed =
CONDITIONAL, dotted = RUMORED/UNKNOWN, and a red `!` marks DANGEROUS. Thick green edges are
implemented story exits. Grey dotted edges to the auxiliary node are counts of documented exits
to places outside the roster.

## Source graph facts (from the 2026-11-04 captures)
- 406 documented directed edges were extracted from the 100 pages' entrance/exit sections. 165+
  of them connect two roster levels.
- Reachability from Level 0 under source rules, ignoring RUMORED and UNKNOWN edges: **48 of 100**
  roster levels. Even with every edge allowed, 49 roster levels (for example 974, 404, 601, 906,
  999 and most sublevels) have **no documented route from Level 0**. They stay standalone,
  developer-accessible destinations until a source-based route exists. No bridge edges were
  invented.
