## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT -> IMPLEMENTATION
- Floating isles in an evening sky -> seeded chunk streaming of sand isles (flat disc/blob slabs with sloped underside), size 5 m to 6 km (compressed), many bridges not needed: moon-like jump gravity (0.15 g) lets the player leap between close isles; fixed low sun, warm evening environment, occasional rain, moderate wind audio.
- Palms: 100-200 per large isle clustered at the middle; coconuts as food item; grass, bushes, crickets as ambience.
- Barrier: invisible stronger-gravity band at ~200 km radius; isles end; loud noise, anxiety (screen vignette). Exit: seeded tiny-probability no-clip while deep in the Barrier to Level 37 or 140; death by falling into the abyss is the risk of jumping.
- Entrance from Level 46: low-gravity ramp area that blends gravity to 0.15.
- Lost Suns settlement on the largest isle as optional anchor (longhouses, tree houses, huts, public house).
- Entity policy: Coconut Snares only (hunting optional, behaviour minimal beyond living on palms). Verticality: isles vary in altitude and inclines; abyss below.
- ABSENT: night, other entities, vehicles.
