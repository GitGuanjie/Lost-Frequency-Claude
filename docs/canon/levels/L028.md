## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: blue-lit grass plain around a central keep, with a Living Abyss approaching, a rotating lightning clock, and a knight guardian.
- Layout: authored keep (2 floors plus basement forge), Stormstone Circle (6 stones) 1.6 km away, graves; seeded grass chunks; mountains ring at the edge with Abyss pools; a forest region (exit to 9.3).
- Light: no sun/moon; faint blue ambient glow outdoors; keep interior in warm lamps; overcast, no rain.
- Audio: wind, distant thunder per strike; hourly strike rhythm.
- Stateful: Abyss exposure meter with 3 stages (memory loss, fever, ash); Almond Water cures stage 1; lightning rotating through 7 zones hourly; Abyss recoils.
- Knight: patrolling NPC, friendly, hurts the Abyss; no attack on player.
- Exits: six ExitTriggers per table above with seeded outcomes where listed.
- Verticality: keep floors and basement; mountains as backdrop.
- ABSENT: rain, sun/moon, hostile entities, other buildings.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the Knight hostile.
- Do not add rain (despite the journal storm).
- Do not spawn smilers/hounds/skin stealers (they disintegrate).
- Do not add more stones than six, nor decode Charity's rune.
- Do not add other buildings (the keep is the only one).
- Do not show suicide on screen.
- Do not link Levels 27 and 29 (navigation only).
