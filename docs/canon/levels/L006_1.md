## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: infinite 1980s-mall food court of restaurants with no-pay food, daylight-lit skylights showing darkness, weekly restocks, rare Level Expansion Events.
- Layout: seeded infinite mall hall chunks with navy chairs and tables; restaurant shells (old-bar wood/red wall variants) as pocket interiors; restaurant exits can lead to a different restaurant (seeded teleport).
- Materials/lighting: mall tile, benches, pillars, plants; skylights with black glass but uniform daylight lighting; orange-white windows.
- Audio: speakers with 80s/90s-like chiptune or synthesized pop, per-bar variation; no entity audio by default.
- State: vending machines give free snacks; weekly restock timer; some restaurants closed until restock; Faceling NPCs helpers.
- Exits: rusty steel door (guarded outpost with 4+ guards), perimeter sliding glass to Level 11, vents to Level 3, bent wall to Blue Channel.
- Event: optional rare Level Expansion Event (wall cracks blue, new halls appear).
- Verticality: none stated.
- ABSENT: hostile spawns by default, wars, payment systems.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make hostile entities natural.
- Do not charge for food or add currency (only "in discussion").
- Do not turn the Guild massacre into a default encounter; it is a logged incident with lockdown.
- Do not put daylight through skylights that show a real sky.
- Do not treat the "free us" line as a documented exit.
- Do not add Level 1.5 or 6.2 edges.
