## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Wake in own home → short authored home interior, then daylight exterior (suburban Earth replica; page names no layout).
- Wrongness grows with time → a stateful "inconsistency" level: lamp posts scale taller, audio delayed against visuals, the sun drifts, clouds speed up, distant animals briefly flicker with wrong skin (shader/cue only).
- Missing items: almond water absent from shops; an in-world map without New Zealand.
- Narrative beach: optional authored beach vignette (sand, shells, wind, waves); keep it labelled as the story frame, not guaranteed geography.
- Exit: speak/confirm "ready to go back" interact → fade, wake in Level 0 (disoriented).
- Entity policy: none. Audio: wind, waves, subtle desync.
- Verticality: none.
- ABSENT: monsters, combat, almond water.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not spawn hounds, fires or faceless things; they are memories.
- Do not make it a hostile level; the sourced hazard is gradual wrongness and loss.
- Do not invent a family; the beach story is narrative.
- Do not treat the reinsertion theory as confirmed.
- Do not supply a map with New Zealand.
