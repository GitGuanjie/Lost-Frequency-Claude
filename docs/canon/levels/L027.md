## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a tiny, safe, warm hot-spring cave that is the last remnant of a destroyed level.
- Layout: single authored cave (about 18.6 m2): pool, two waterfalls, stairway at a corner, small drain tunnel (too small to enter), second waterfall opening high on the cliff wall.
- Materials: limestone, warm potable water, humid air, no life. Audio: water drips and waterfalls, cave reverb; fog/humidity.
- Mechanic: bathing timer; at about 1 hour a calm/optimism buff; past 2 hours dizziness (screen sway). Crowd cap about 30 is lore only (no NPCs required).
- Exits: stairs trigger up to the tunnel (walk back to origin); optional secret "other waterfall" climb leading to a short tunnel, cavern and black Stormstone plain (log) ending in a one-way fade to Level 28; mark as DANGEROUS.
- Verticality: modest; cliff climb is optional.
- Entities: none. ABSENT: bacteria/plants/fish, combat, bases.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add entities, plants, fish or algae.
- Do not present the Stormstone plain as part of the safe level; it is TESTIMONY and the page calls it "nowhere".
- Do not add communities or outposts.
- Do not make the drain tunnel traversable; it is too small.
- Do not treat the log's Stormstone detonation as a confirmed hazard of the spring cave.
