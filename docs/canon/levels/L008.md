## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: non-Euclidean caves, shifting gravity, flooding, entropy.
- Layout grammar: authored 9th Road spine (pool start, markers every 50 m, M.E.G. outposts, Harmouth camp) with seeded chunk caves branching; rock shader plus almond-water volumes.
- Defining mechanic: flood zones (water level changes), swim oxygen; air quality; cold; markers degrade over time (entropy) unless watched.
- Gravity change per cavern: flip or tilt gravity vector in authored cave rooms; use sparingly.
- Exits: Level 9 at road end (walk_in); alternate triggers as seeded rare (vent, noclip, floor fall, tar).
- Audio: dripping, echo, reverb long, chittering; dim lamps.
- Entities: sourced only; spiders swarm zones, tar pit hands, wrangler as audio/tremor event; keep non-combat.
- Vertical shafts to climb (ladders) allowed; floating rocks.
- ABSENT: any invented named NPCs beyond sourced factions.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make it a wholly flat tunnel maze; it is non-Euclidean with gravity changes.
- Do not make Almond water harmful to drink; guide says generally safe.
- Do not make 9th Road lead anywhere but Level 9 as primary exit (Dark Highway continues to Level 11 elsewhere).
- Do not treat guide-voice jokes as hard rules unless sourced.
- Do not remove the Veiled's absence: they were eliminated; only ruins remain.
- Do not place Level 7 or 480 edges (links appear without a stated edge).
