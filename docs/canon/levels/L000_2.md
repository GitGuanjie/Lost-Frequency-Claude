## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: pristine remodeled Level 0 collapses on entry, revealing an unfinished house; resets on re-entry.
- Layout: small authored set of rooms from the entrance door outward; pristine state on spawn. Chain-reaction collapse propagates from entrance toward the exit door (SOURCE: "chain reaction of destruction reaches the door back to Level 0").
- Materials: dry red carpet, white painted plaster, ceiling tile, outlets; after collapse: raw studs, concrete slab, unfinished-house framing. Dust particles/fog increase after collapse.
- Hazard: falling-debris zones (damage) and dust (reduced visibility/health tick), per source.
- Reset: leaving through the door and re-entering rebuilds the pristine state.
- Exit: door trigger to Level 0 at the far end of the collapse path.
- Documents: three readable notes (Docs 1-3), the third ending mid-sentence.
- Verticality: floor collapse reveals the house but no deeper level is stated; keep within one storey.
- Entities: none. ABSENT: crew NPCs, combat, communities.
