## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: infinite but only the trench system accessible; complex Euclidean layout with random twists -> seeded chunk-streamed trench network (corridors with wooden plank/iron sheet/sandbag walls, mud floor, rusted wire) with authored anchors (gun emplacements, artillery stations, bunkers, the three Gutter Bunkers).
- The hard rule: parapet top is a kill boundary. Anything fully above ground that is a human-sized heat source is killed by a sniper shot; escalate to missile/artillery if armed/armoured; a doll decoy is ignored. Implement as an invisible exit-line check, no visible enemy.
- Weather states: extended day/night (~2x), thick fog (visibility/audio loss), rainstorm with waist-deep flooding and lightning (random strike exit to L3) and a flooded tunnel (L7). Random shelling at intervals; rare bomber with a L.E.A.D.S. prompt.
- Entity: Trenchcleaner as a slow stalking agent with blue lights and head beam, sees by heat, fires chaingun on line of sight; silence/low/cover rules. One to three instances may be placed; text conflict means keep to one with optional second as rumour.
- Exits: rusted bunker doors in Gutter Bunkers (L14/65/50), lightning (L3), flood tunnel (L7), wanderer-kill (L666, one-way, NPC-less so only via player-kill hostile animation).
- Notes/logs: general's letters, sergeant's letter, Lancaster log as examine items flagged as testimony; Almond Water, canned food, medical kits as pickups.
- Verticality: none beyond trench depth; underground tunnels connect Gutter Bunkers. Lighting: day/night cycle, fog, red-blue entity glow. Audio: silence, distant gunfire (reduced at night), shell whistles, mechanical whine.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not allow leaving the trench; the page says it is invariably fatal and exits are not via the surface.
- Do not give the unseen entities visible models; they are never seen.
- Do not make the level a combat shooter; 'Fight back' is on the DON'T list.
- Do not make the B.F.G. or M.E.G. outposts safe hubs with trade systems beyond the page; keep them minimal.
- Do not treat Nuvaria/Hagsten as confirmed; they are hypotheses and in-universe documents.
- Do not add Level 48 as an edge; it appears only in the link list.
- Do not give a single canonical Trenchcleaner count; testimonies conflict.
