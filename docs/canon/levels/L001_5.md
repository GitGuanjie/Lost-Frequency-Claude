## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: inverted lighting, near silence, whispers that fade as you approach.
- Layout grammar: abstract white-walled rooms and halls (walls "blank, white", TESTIMONY) with a few authored anchors: a window, a door. Page gives no geometry, so keep compact.
- Lighting: ceiling emissive panels that darken (negative light), ambient light fill from "shadows"; invert palette. Possible use of unlit shader and inverted colour grade.
- Audio: no ballast hum (article); whisper bed with proximity-inverted volume, so quieter near the source; near-silence.
- Stateful mechanic: whisper-volume proximity cue; silence means danger.
- Exit: noclip trigger (rare, seeded) with unspecified destination; door in deeper corridor as optional one-way narrative end.
- Entities: whisper sources only as audio emitters; no visible combat actor. Mother/husk only as scripted audio/touch events if included.
- Verticality: none stated; image is upside down but no geometry claim.
- ABSENT: bases, communities, items.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add hum (article says absent; Log 3 says quiet hum, an internal conflict).
- Do not add bases, outposts or NPC communities.
- Do not make windows a reliable way out; they are entry traps.
- Do not give explicit Level-1 exit.
- Do not treat Log 7 as reliable.
- Do not render texts as readable garbled puzzles requiring other logs.
