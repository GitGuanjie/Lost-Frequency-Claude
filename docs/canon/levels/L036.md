## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE FACT: arrival on a conveyor belt at baggage claim with a single bounded wall -> spawn on a moving belt (scripted carrier) at carousel numbered per origin level; endless rows of carousels along a wall; ceramic tile, steel, fluorescent panels.
- SOURCE FACT: infinite horizontal check-in then non-Euclidean maze -> check-in hall as straight strip; beyond it, concourse chunks with seeded self-connecting corridors (portal/teleport loops) and contradictory signs; avoid true spatial overlap complexity - use teleport seams.
- SOURCE FACT: flight countdown screens, PA announcements -> shared global tick counter (unitless), screens updated, PA synth voice; gates open when countdown reaches 1000 and close at 0.
- SOURCE FACT: time dilation -> player timekeeper items are frozen; no sleep action; no day/night; add a mild slow-heal HUD perception cue (not extra damage).
- SOURCE FACT: Meeting Place appears in 47 positions -> one seeded recurring lounge landmark that reappears in several chunks.
- Exits: gate door ExitTrigger to Sublevel 36.1 (separate level); catalogued flights give conditional exits to 828, 750, 186.
- Verticality: not stated beyond concourses/terminals. Entity policy: no hostile entities; optional peaceful NPC-less ambience.
