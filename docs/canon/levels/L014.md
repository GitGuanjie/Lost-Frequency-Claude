## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a beautiful night forest that talks the visitor into staying; the exit is deliberately not given.
- Layout grammar: small authored night-forest basin with waterfalls and crimson grass; procedural filler is optional. Rows of small figures at the edge of sight are authored anchors, kept at distance.
- Materials/lighting/audio: crimson grass, dark trees, starlit unlit sky, moonlight, waterfall audio overlaid with whispering and a cacophony that rises near the figures.
- Stateful mechanic: a persuasion meter (reading/approaching figures lowers control clarity); resisting means walking away. No combat.
- Exits: no literal exit trigger. Implementable as UNKNOWN: the way back is the player's own conditions (e.g. turn away repeatedly); mark as non-canon helper if added.
- Verticality: none required; waterfalls are scenery.
- Entity policy: NO ENTITY PER SOURCE; avoid chasers.
- ABSENT: bases, items, quests, any real exit sign.
