## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a safe, hospitable library ruled by Blanche, with etiquette-based entry and exit.
- Layout grammar: endless-feeling library of wooden shelves, floating shelves with ladders, blue velvet carpet with gold, candelabra lighting; authored anchors: Blanche's Office (centre, entry), Tea Room (central kitchen), Guest Rooms (variable); procedural shelf aisles seeded around them.
- Ceiling: very tall, ornamented with stained glass; fake depth so it cannot be reached (distance cheat or high far plane).
- Materials: wood, carpet, glass, painted ceiling, candlelit emissive points; warm fog-free air.
- Audio: quiet room tone, page sounds, harpsichord/organ sheet-music vibe is only inferred from sheets (do not claim music is playing).
- Mechanic: etiquette system: polite dialogue keeps tea invitation; rude actions give warnings, lecture, immobilisation, then teleport out; recording tools (camera feature) malfunction.
- Exits: ask Blanche (select a safe level) or rude expulsion.
- Entrance: signature interaction on a book.
- Entities: Blanche, Tom (guest-room occupant, optional), Light Guides floating erratically.
- Verticality: ladders to floating shelves; ceiling unreachable.
- ABSENT: hostile entities, bases.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Blanche hostile or combative; hostility is met with immobilisation then expulsion.
- Do not add hazards, gore or "Witnessing the Beyond" as a real mechanic (unconfirmed rumour).
- Do not let photographed signatures enter the level.
- Do not let Blanche guide to unsafe levels.
- Do not add bases or other communities.
- Do not reproduce the guest-book titles beyond a few generic shelves.
