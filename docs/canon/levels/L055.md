## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: infinite segmented abandoned restaurant, Safe Room as sole entry -> authored Safe Room (a restaurant dining room, brick, wallpaper, wood floor) plus a seeded outskirts network of restaurant rooms (counter, kitchen, seating; rare bathrooms) via chunk streaming.
- Layout shifts: when the player is not in the outskirts (e.g. in Safe Room or on a respawn), reseed room adjacency without adding new rooms; the return from Safe Room yields a different arrangement each time.
- Materials/lighting/audio: cracked brick, flaking wallpaper, floorboards, dust; sun-shaft light beams from unseen sources at various angles (no windows, no sun object); NO light hum, NO machinery; very quiet room tone only; humid 21 C feel (fog light, no particles).
- Stateful mechanic: hidden stay timer (random duration, short 53 min to long) that ends with an automatic return to the entry level, with no re-entry flag; optional in-game hint that the player 'knows' time is limited.
- Effects: sunlight causes drowsiness for exhausted players (fatigue relief or sleep prompt); hallucinations from long stays (subtle audio/visual, no entities).
- Food: Frontrooms meals in kitchens, plain water; no Almond Water.
- Exit: timed ExitTrigger back to the entry level; the page names no other exit.
- Verticality: none. Entities: none. M.E.G. supply caches (phone, pamphlets) as examine items only.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add entities or chasers; the level has no signs of life.
- Do not add Almond Water or Backrooms foods.
- Do not add ordinary exit doors or let the player choose to leave.
- Do not allow re-entry after leaving.
- Do not show a visible sun or windows; the light source is imperceptible.
- Do not treat the narrator's memory scenes as article fact (TESTIMONY/fiction).
- Do not add hum or machinery sounds; silence is the identity.
