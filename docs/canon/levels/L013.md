## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: monotone apartment complex, 290 floors, infinite/looping -> seeded chunk streaming of identical corridors, doors and 3-4 room flats (kitchen-living, bedroom, bathroom); plaster/laminate/linoleum shaders; dim lights.
- SOURCE: rooms regenerate stock for Earthborn -> per-flat fridge restock on re-entry (basic meals), TV with out-of-order vintage media, old books; low-risk survival loop.
- SOURCE: lethargy and attachment -> stateful 'room attachment' meter that rises while resting in a claimed room and makes leaving slower/harder; vanishings only as an unexplained, non-entity fade for >10 in-game-day residents (no cause shown).
- Exits: ordinary-looking doors (L0/114/327/395/410) as seeded ExitTriggers; bottom-floor stairs to L70/208; chessboard+two-chairs room spawn (interact); '235' door; red door; yellow wall noclip; endless stair to L280.
- Verticality: stairs and elevators between floors with inconsistent elevators; bottom-floor stair exits; topmost-floor entry from Level 12. Keep as streamed floors, not a visible tower.
- Entities: NONE. Hazard is non-entity (lethargy/vanishing). Optional narrative notes of the interview as readable testimony, flagged unreliable.
- Addendum catastrophe (12% destroyed, 41% damaged) is time-stamped lore; leave out of base build or gate behind a flag.
