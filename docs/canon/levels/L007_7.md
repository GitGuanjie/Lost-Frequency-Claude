## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT -> IMPLEMENTATION
- Solitary rock with lighthouse over an endless calm ocean -> authored fixed scene: narrow cylindrical rock slab, 47 m up to lighthouse (concrete, wood, rust), ocean water volume to horizon (deterministic, no chunking needed), flat gray unlit-sky environment with constant daylight, constant moderate wind, wave audio only.
- Lighthouse interior: ground floor (dim, light from stairs/door), upper floor (windows, rusted pipes, sealed gas pipes), exterior roof with Lamp Zone. Interior may be larger than the exterior.
- Defining stateful mechanic: a hidden pressure counter (valve ticking up) leads to panel implosion -> gas leak (poison/blur/bleeding status) -> Lamp Zone explosion and fire. Treat as hypothesis from audio log; implement as timed event that the player can influence only via the valve, whose effect the page leaves unanswered (keep it as "not stated", e.g. nothing known).
- Exit: jump from the rock edge into water volume (fall damage risk by seed), swim with oxygen/stamina for several hours of in-game minutes (compress) past a "rock out of sight" distance to trigger transition; no marker.
- Verticality: lighthouse 47 m above water; rock extends below.
- Entity policy: none. ABSENT: other people, birds, fish, bases, wildlife, shelter, food.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add sea creatures or birds; the level is devoid of life.
- Do not make the control panels or radio functional; they are beyond repair (only a testimony hypothesis of one-time pulses).
- Do not claim shutting the valve prevents the disaster; the page withholds this.
- Do not add a day-night cycle or clouds.
- Do not let two players coexist.
- Do not make the descent easy or give ropes; Bob Alley's dropped gear is only mentioned.
