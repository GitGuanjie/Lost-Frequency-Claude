## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: exact copy of Level 37 geometry with red-lit, darkening, viscous water -> reuse the L037 tile pool generator with a different environment preset (red-tinged water, near-black ambient, emissive red sprouts as sparse light sources).
- Stateful mechanic: a progressive 'transition' from L037: light sources attenuate while the player walks in, water becomes viscous (slowed swimming), then a point of no return where retracing steps is impossible; this gives the sense of unnoticed crossing.
- Audio: faint distant chant chorus (synth pad voices, quiet), slithering and whimper cues rarely, water thick and slow. Reverb inherited from L037.
- Entities: Ambassadors only as fast hovering silhouettes that ignore the player. The 'elusive entity' is audio-only, never visible; the multi-limb figure may appear once as a corrupted camera frame item (no chaser).
- Items: a found damaged camera (Suraj C.) and its transcript as an examine note (flag it as testimony).
- Rumoured water effects (rejuvenation, foresight, healing) may be shown as unverified conflicting notes, never as guaranteed mechanics.
- Exits: not stated in the source. Do not invent them; provide a placeholder 'no known exit' state or mark exit UNKNOWN for the owner to decide.
- Verticality: same as L037 (stairs descending into pits); none added.
