## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Four mineral zones, each its own geometry → separate authored cave chunks: Zone 1 smooth 15x17 ft cylindrical tunnels with turquoise emissive crystals and Meditation Cove; Zone 2 irregular 7x5 ft tunnels, brittle rock, dim amethyst light; Zone 3 10x10 ft tunnels with steep inclines and red glow, plus the Crimson Fortress; Zone 4 black cave (optional, flagged unconfirmed).
- Calming crystals (Zone 1): stress/panic reduction; turquoise pickups carried forward.
- Amethyst wall sealing: slow-growing barrier; going back in time works; going forward turns the tunnel into a dead-end disease pocket.
- Disease and heat: Zone 2 infection timer (gas mask and Almond Water mitigate); Zone 3 heat/burn on touching glowing rubies.
- Turquoise-as-tool: enraged entity trance; used crystal detonates after a delay.
- Verticality: Zone 2 second layer via floor break (~10 ft fall); Zone 3 83-degree inclines; Crimson Fortress with 1000 ft bridges.
- Entity policy: per zone as sourced; none in Zone 1 beyond rumours; Entranced as passive NPC group.
- ABSENT: any confirmed Zone 4 content treated as fact.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not treat Zone 4 or the Dark Sovereign as confirmed.
- Do not let Zone 1 have entities (only two unconfirmed Smiler reports).
- Do not allow turquoise to work on all entities; only Wretches and Crawlers calm via looking through it.
- Do not let water leave the level.
- Do not use the Supplementary zone; it is not in the snapshot.
- Do not make the Crimson Fortress a repeatable structure; leaving means you will rarely see one again.
