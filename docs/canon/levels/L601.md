## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a silent endless library that loops and traps, with personalised fake realities.
- Layout grammar: modern library (shelves, carpet, ceiling tile) in a seeded repeating grid with a central authored area: metal letters "the end is near" and loud humming computer terminals.
- Materials: carpet, shelving wood, ceiling tile; terminals emissive.
- Audio: near-silent room tone (about -6.2 dB areas); terminal buzz only in centre.
- Mechanic: loop/wall trick (walking one way loops; turning around meets a wall); electric devices disabled (flashlight policy: off, since "anything that uses electricity ... ceases"); the player cannot perceive others (no NPCs shown).
- Fake realities: small authored pockets blending with the library at borders; breaching restores the library; a rare pocket contains an exit (seeded chance).
- Entrance trigger: neon-sign door from other levels.
- Entities: Partygoers only as sourced threat, optional; "host" only mentioned.
- Verticality: none stated.
- ABSENT: working electronics (except terminals), reliable exit.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not give a reliable exit or let noclip exit.
- Do not allow flashlights or radios to work.
- Do not make the terminals safe to use.
- Do not turn the Junkies into friendly vendors without Memory Juice.
- Do not treat the narrator's claims as verified.
- Do not name the "host" entity.
