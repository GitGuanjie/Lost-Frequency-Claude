## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Arrive by water: spawn in shallow (4 ft) sea, wade to Lower Area dirt path.
- Layered strip: dark concrete bay walk (upper) over dirt construction path (lower); stairs ~100 m apart with lit lamps; most lamps dead; seeded chunk streaming along the bay.
- Painless Death: stateful calm meter when the player faces the breeze near water; rises with time and anxiety; breaks by a partner/slap or by moving; at max, death. Stops at Low-Tide.
- Sea states: seeded timed cycle of Normal / Portal / Low-Tide / Ocean; Portal sends to Level 7 with probability; Ocean has downward current drowning.
- Smilers: scripted light-attracted agents only in the Upper Area; none in the Lower Area.
- Audio: sea wind and waves, lamp buzz; no sun.
- Verticality: just two tiers connected by stairs; sea alongside.
- ABSENT: daylight, food/water in Upper Area, entities in Lower Area.

## Canon traps (do not)
- No day cycle.
- Do not place Smilers in the Lower Area.
- Do not make Painless Death a universal effect; it requires fear/anxiety.
- Do not treat the Level 61 sleep exit as confirmed.
- Do not give a leader to The Brave.
