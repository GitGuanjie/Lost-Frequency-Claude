## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT -> IMPLEMENTATION
- Four room grammars -> authored templates stitched via seeded graph: Waterpark hall (slides, dry pools, blue plastic terrain), Corridor (metal plate floor, MDF ceiling, scarce lights), Cafe (one door, vending machine, till), Storeroom (shelves, crates, fluorescent blackouts). Storerooms 3x Waterparks frequency.
- Metal floor noise -> footstep loudness raises an alert meter that draws Smilers/Dullers in Corridors (sourced behaviour: noise attracts entities).
- Tegan Fruit stages -> fruit items with time-stage flag; ripe gives "plastic" status (defensive layer, no aging), elder harms.
- Vending machines: free, 10 of each item, slow restock; opened machine never restocks.
- Level Key item, flyer interact, crate entrance/exit as ExitTriggers; black slide to 58.1 flagged dangerous.
- Tribe and B.N.T.G. base: optional zones; tribe are doll-like NPCs speaking Welsh-translated lines.
- Audio: clanking metal steps, fluorescent hum, quiet dripless room tone; lighting: dim, dark corridors, flashlight needed in cafes.
- Verticality: waterpark slides only (decorative, collapse risk). No water except Almond Water poured in the tribe's pools.
- ABSENT: filled pools, real exterior, other entity types.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not fill the pools with water; they are dry (except tribe pools with Almond Water).
- Do not add Hounds or other entities; page lists only Smilers, Dullers, Windows and one Wretch.
- Do not invent the starting room differently; all wanderers share the main Waterpark.
- Do not make cafe vending machines charge or restock reliably.
- Do not claim Level 57/59 connections; links lack context.
- Do not make the tribe hostile; they are described as friendly neighbours of B.N.T.G.
