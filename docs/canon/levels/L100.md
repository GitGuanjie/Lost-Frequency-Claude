## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: an endless, motionless strip of sand and sea with periodic graves, tended by a gentle tall being, left only by being carried away after exhausting oneself in the water.
- Layout grammar: a long thin straight sand spit (fixed width, seeded endless length) in a water volume that extends to the horizon. Authored anchors: grave clusters (single, then three, then the working site), repeated at long distance.
- Materials/lighting: pale sand, turquoise water shader with small waves only near shore, still glass-like sea beyond, clear bright sun, no clouds; no day-night change stated.
- Audio: soft lapping near shore, stagnant wind (very low), distant silence.
- Stateful mechanic: swim stamina/oxygen drains; wandering far ahead yields graves; drowning does not kill but triggers the being scene.
- Exit: swim out until oxygen fails, fade to black, being appears, short interaction, transition to Level 4 (Base Omega) as an ONE-WAY edge; no way back to Earth.
- Verticality: none.
- Entity policy: one non-hostile tall black being only; it nods, never speaks.
- ABSENT: hostile entities, bases, items, any island or second landmass, weather, time-of-day change.
- Content handling: do not depict the cliff jump or the father scenes; start in the sand state. Epitaph text may be paraphrased; keep dates within 2018.
