## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: submerged tiled complex -> authored tile pools, halls, irregular pits, pillars in water, plus seeded procedural fill; water volumes at lukewarm, wading to deep; ceramic tile shader (pristine, identical).
- SOURCE: no ledges, stairs into deep pits -> deep water volumes need swim with buoyancy and oxygen; add exits from water only where not contradicted (keep some ledges absent, but ensure playable ways out).
- SOURCE: irregular light, dark corners -> sparse emissive panels at odd angles with real dark zones; flashlight allowed; blue-green fog/underwater tint.
- Audio: altered, muffled sound; reverb off/dry; water sound that is quiet or occasionally obtrusive (seeded variant per session).
- Stateful mechanic: Epsom-salt water has a relaxing effect after extended immersion (lower stress/pain meter, slows sprint) with no damage; constant tiny ripple shader.
- Exits: water immersion (seeded) to L130, dark enclosed tunnels to L43, rare corridors to L233; entrances from L58/L119/L130/L233/L25 by water, tile areas, arcade.
- Verticality: stairs descending into water pits; no tower. Entity policy: none, no NPCs, no outposts.
