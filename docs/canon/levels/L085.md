## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: Dead End exit; polluted plastic Earth parody; sheltered luxury mansions; radio Station Number One; RoboPets in some mansions.
- Layout: seeded streamed chunks of three biomes (grass with plastic blades, hills, highway slabs); raised climate-controlled buildings (mansion, supermarket, warehouse) as anchors; ocean with acidic water volumes and beaches.
- Weather/time: 24 h cycle, rare big storms with flooding, smog fog tiers, heatwaves, UV exposure meter; damage outdoors over time.
- Interior: lavish rooms at 21 C, working lights, solar-powered; fake plastic food props; Royal Rations/Star Candy.
- Audio: Station Number One radios (music, adverts, numbers, rare siren and red flashing), 110 dB option.
- Entities: RoboPets only in ~10% of mansions; recharging logic; cannot open doors.
- Exit: only a rare unintentional noclip ExitTrigger (low seeded chance with no player-initiated way); intentional noclip must not work. Vehicle driving allowed.
- Verticality: buildings raised; hills have mines/tunnels (finite depth not given).
- ABSENT: biological animals, safe outdoors, easy exit, active M.E.G. outpost (all dead).

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add a working exit button, warpberries or deliberate noclips.
- Do not populate with animals or Backrooms entities other than RoboPets.
- Do not make taps run clean.
- Do not give signal to outside via devices.
- Do not make the screaming broadcast permanent: it ran 2019 only.
- Do not mark the military base content (redacted).
