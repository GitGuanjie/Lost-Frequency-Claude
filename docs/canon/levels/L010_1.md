## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Sub-section is only a lake in Level 10, 40 km from Malt Town → compact outdoor basin: dark crimson viscous surface, banks with grass and algae, barbed-wire perimeter with warning signs; no buildings.
- Radiation 7 Sv/h; death within the hour (stage timers 10/30/60 min) → exposure clock once inside; hazard overlay, Geiger-like audio (credits mention a Geiger sample), no cure; death ends the run rather than gore.
- Contact drains blood; plants unaffected → shoreline contact trigger ends run; plants shown safe.
- Exclusion Zone: crossing fence = stated certain death (nobody rescues you).
- Exit: none safe; depression under lake as ONE-WAY/unknown (the page says no returns).
- Entity policy: no agents; the lake surface may react (tendrils) only if kept minimal and stateful.
- Verticality: none (open basin).
- ABSENT: creatures, loot, safe base, cure.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not give protective suits as a solution: page says even suited contact means death within the hour.
- Do not stage Malt Town as part of the level; it is 40 km away.
- Do not add monsters or fish; there is no wildlife besides plants and algae.
- Do not show gore beyond what the game rating allows; death can be implied.
- Do not make the lake's undocumented level a normal exit.
