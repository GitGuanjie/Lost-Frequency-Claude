## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a collapsed, many-storey car park of a dead micronation; unstable rubble; deep mining holes; corpses in tents; floors stratified by wealth.
- Layout grammar: stacked parking-deck slabs (authored 28ish levels not required; render fewer), broken floors with large shafts, rubble piles, girders, rust cars, tents and shacks.
- Materials: concrete (cracked), rebar, rusted car husks, canvas tents; dust; sparse remnants of top-floor luxury (pool/football table) as a deliberate upper anchor.
- Lighting/audio: dim, dusty light; wind through voids, creaking concrete, falling debris; no hum is stated.
- Mechanic: instability, tripping on rubble/rebar, fall hazard; climbing holes risky (fatal accident chance); readable diary/census notes as examine items.
- Exits: fall/climb through holes to Level 21/23; noclip through cars/debris to Level 817.
- Verticality: strong, multi-deck with shafts.
- Entity policy: none; no hostile spawns.
- ABSENT: living community, trade, Wi-Fi activity.
