## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Healing level vs corrupted level: build the White Halls state as the playable level (a single long hallway with branches to sixteen rooms, white tile, regenerating surfaces). The Decay state is a second variant (seeded flag) with black pits and caved walls.
- Detox Zones: authored trigger volumes in empty rooms that gradually restore health with a pain/burn cue (screen pulse, no instant heal). Medical-supply rooms hold bandage/saline-style flags only if wanted (items as "medical supplies" are sourced generically).
- Decay variant: Detox Zone becomes a trap where wound closure reverses (health drops after an initial heal); black pits as hole volumes where thrown items vanish (line test is a sourced beat).
- Lighting: blinding white emissive panels; audio is not stated, so use sparse room tone and a clean-chemical ambience (the "sharp smell of cleaning chemicals" is TESTIMONY, smell only).
- Exits: both hallway ends are noclip triggers back to Level 1; entrance is not an in-level concern.
- Verticality: none. Entity policy: none; M.E.G. doctors/guards as non-hostile NPCs optional.
- ABSENT: monsters, rooms beyond sixteen, outdoors.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the level a combat zone; the hazard is Decay, not creatures.
- Do not treat Decay state as the only state; the healing level is the article's description.
- Do not let Detox Zones cure congenital illness.
- Do not claim the Decay narrator's smell or pits as the original appearance.
- Do not invent a Level 1.2 or "Level √2" connection; only link targets exist (/level-1-2, /level-1-4).
