## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: safe, ticketed M.E.O.D. theme-park resort; seven themed zones with compact Non-Euclidean interiors; Dark Alley loops pursuers.
- Layout grammar: authored anchors (car park, plaza with turnstile row and archway row, Main Plaza street, Folding Point shell with downscaled Level 0 recreation, Piering In pier/fairground, Dark Alley, Mountain edge, High Street) with procedural fill of stalls and shop fronts. Keep the park about 200 m across.
- Materials/lighting/audio: asphalt car park, paving, wood pier, sand/pebbles, glass and concrete Folding Point, grass; bright daylight, retro ambient speaker music, crowd murmur, rain only at closing time.
- Stateful mechanic: ticket check at turnstiles (item flag); optional Dark Alley loop teleport that sets the player back several metres (seamless re-entry of the same corridor); shop interiors larger than their facades.
- Exits: walk through a labelled brick archway (walk_in), one per parent level listed; choose the entry archway as spawn.
- Verticality: Folding Point five floors + basement, wooden pier platform, mountain trails; keep modest.
- Entity policy: only sourced staff/mascot NPCs (static or simple wanderers) and no hostile agents.
- Must stay ABSENT: InfoMan booth, Facelings, Mr. Bones coaster, hostile entities, ruin state (unless a variant is deliberately chosen).

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not treat offset 0 (old Level 32 wayback text) as the current level.
- Do not turn the abusive-relationship recordings into gameplay; they are narrative frame and carry a content warning.
- Do not apply Non-Euclidean effects to the Kiddie Zone.
- Do not make the level dangerous or entity-driven; the page gives no class and a "No Harmful Entities" banner.
- Do not assume a closed or ruined park unless modelling the explicit 2040/2067 variants.
