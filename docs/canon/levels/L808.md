## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a glowing low-poly synthwave continent where the player's body is a light and a city of a recruiting group is the safe hub.
- Layout grammar: authored central entrance gate, with four anchors by direction: Retro-seaboard (SE), Art Hall (30 km N, scaled), Infofield (SW), Sunset City valley (2 km W); wireframe grid ground between them.
- Materials/lighting/audio: unlit gridline ground shader, neon emissives, pink particle beach; fixed low sunset; day/night as grid colour swap magenta to dark green; synth pads/chiptune.
- Stateful mechanic: player body emits neon light (dynamic light pool); night = grid recolour and dimmer sun plus occasional beams.
- Exits: TRANSIT subway station in Sunset City to Level 185 (the only exit).
- Verticality: city valley buildings; no tall tower required.
- Entity policy: low-poly polygon entities as non-hostile ambient agents; group-aligned facelings/hounds/deathmoths only inside the city, not hostile.
- ABSENT: realistic nature, natural weather, M.E.G. base.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Backrooms Entertainment hostile; difficulty is 0.
- Do not use real sunrise/sunset movement; the sun is stuck at sunset.
- Do not make Infofield windows solid or permanent.
- Do not add a second numbered exit; only the TRANSIT subway is stated.
- Do not paint the Retro-seaboard dive as safe; a giant light lurks 200 m down.
