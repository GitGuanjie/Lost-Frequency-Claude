## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: arrival always at the base of a concrete office stairwell -> a short authored stair (eight flights), a small room with a skylight and a pile of painted pebbles (~38), and an empty doorway leading outward.
- SOURCE: flat cloud plane, wool-textured, with depth illusion -> large flat plane with a cloud-wool shader (parallax depth by view angle); no props; seeded distance bands from the epicentre: 0-30 km white, 30-80 km grey with cold breeze, 80+ km black haze and gales, then a storm wall 15 km in.
- Distance as the stateful mechanic: compress the scale (e.g. accelerated travel/odometer) so the player feels the zone changes; temperature 11 C feel via fog and breath cues; wind up to 29-31 m/s reduces speed and pushes the player; rain.
- Entities: only sparse, slow drifters moving outward from the epicentre that ignore the player unless provoked (Smiler, Duller, Woodlin allowed in grey zone); black zone animalistic types are rumoured so keep optional and rare.
- Camera/flashlight gear degrade in the storm (waterlogged within minutes) as the signature hazard.
- Exit: none. The storm wall is the end; implement as a non-graphic fade to credits/ending state with no destination. Do not add a way back through the doorway unless the owner decides; the page does not forbid retreat.
- Content warning screen about suicide themes is required at level start; no depictions of methods, no gore. Verticality: only the eight stair flights; otherwise flat.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not build a destination beyond the storm; the page says it is unknown.
- Do not make entities hostile packs; they ignore wanderers unless provoked.
- Do not populate the plane with landmarks or structures.
- Do not depict suicide methods or gore; keep an end-state abstract with a content warning.
- Do not change the arrival: it is always the stairwell base, never on the plane.
- Do not treat hypotheses A and B as article-level fact; both are stated as ideas.
- Do not tie the level to a specific entrance origin list beyond the white double door.
