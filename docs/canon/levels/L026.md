## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a real-world-looking cruise ship (exterior) whose lower decks are infinite; moves by green-fog no-clip; exits via reversed poster or jumping overboard.
- Layout grammar: authored ship exterior (hull ~150 x 18 m, three upper decks, crimson pool, bar, lounge, sun deck). Procedural interior: seeded infinite lower-deck corridors from an elevator hub, themed party rooms, cloned rooms.
- Materials/lighting: trashed deck wood, metal, carpet, blood decals; party lights; green fog for transition.
- Audio: echoing screams (distant), party music/muffled, creaking ship, sea if above water.
- Stateful mechanic: periodic green fog; ship relocates (environment swap). Poster pickups: TIME 4 FUN style (smiley-less) may no-clip in; reversed unfocused poster exits.
- Exits: reversed poster interact to Level 4; overboard (leave undefined or lethal per design note); do not add other exits.
- Verticality: decks (moderate) plus elevator descent to infinite lower decks; the sun deck is the top ("nowhere higher to go").
- Entity policy: Partygoers dominant (stalking/trapping), Carnivorous Balloons in dark corners. Torture/gore at a toned-down level or flagged.
- ABSENT: friendly bases, safe zone.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the interior equal the ship's exterior size; it is infinite.
- Do not add a friendly faction or shop.
- Do not treat the Philadelphia Experiment essay as level mechanics beyond the "magnetized" lore.
- Do not claim multiple witnesses escaped; April Showers is described as "the only wanderer to escape".
- Do not make the stone circle part of this level without noting the ambiguity.
