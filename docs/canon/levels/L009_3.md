## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a static, foggy Möbius landscape with an approaching-but-never-arriving engine sound and no exit.
- Layout grammar: a looping field of tall wheat corridors, rolling hills, a single highway, a few hilltop wooden houses; seeded chunk streaming with wrap-around (e.g. world-space modulo) so forests always lead back to fields.
- Materials: grass, wheat (tall), asphalt, wood (wet), brick with moss and steel net; heavy fog environment with short visibility; overcast soft light.
- Audio: wind weak, damp ambience, distant engine drone that rises but never resolves; no animals.
- Stateful mechanic: engine proximity cue (volume swells then falls, never peaks); player may crouch in fog/bushes (the page says fog is a blanket to hide under).
- Static: no growth/decay, no day cycle, no weather change.
- Interior: wooden house with bathroom and casement window onto trees; back door weak; passing leads to fields.
- Exit: none; the only edge is the loop. Entry edge from Level 9 as story.
- Verticality: gentle hills only; no towers.
- Entities: none.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add monsters, NPCs, animals or insects.

- Do not make the engine ever appear or reach the player.
- Do not add time progression or decay.
- Do not make the fog a random hazard; it is orientation-defeating.
