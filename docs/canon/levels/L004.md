## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: empty office, nearly no furniture -> authored anchor rooms (plain office rooms, corridors, ceiling-tile, carpet/concrete) plus seeded procedural fill; keep furniture near zero.
- SOURCE: most windows blacked out; unblacked windows are traps -> windows as black panels; a rare clear window as an examine-only warning hazard with no scripted entity (page gives none).
- SOURCE: Almond Water in coolers, vending machines, fountains -> interactable dispensers that grant an 'almond water' inventory flag; abundant.
- SOURCE: lots of wanderers/communities -> optional static NPC-less outposts (M.E.G. Base Omega, Amor Incrementum, T.B.D.) as signage/notes only; no trade system required.
- Exits: office stairwell ExitTriggers to L5 and L6 (common), elevator to L3 (seeded chance), rare basement stair to L71; line-of-sight loss may despawn exit (seeded).
- Entities: Hounds and Dullers may be sourced but have no behaviour text; prefer none or ambient audio only. Smiler must stay absent (unverified rumour).
- Verticality: none beyond stairs/elevator exits; no towers. Lighting/audio unspecified: use neutral office ballast hum, flashlight policy default.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the level hostile or entity-heavy; page says it is mostly devoid of entities.
- Do not add a Smiler as confirmed.
- Do not invent room layout lore, lighting colour, or hours; page is silent.
- Do not make exits permanent: they can disappear out of line of sight.
- Do not treat page banner placeholders as a survival class.
