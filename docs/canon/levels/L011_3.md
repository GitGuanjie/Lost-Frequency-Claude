## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: psychoactive red air; empty gutted city; Servants stalk and herd then capture; bridges out are guarded.
- Layout grammar: dense/sparse tiers from outskirts to core (boxes for low-rise to towers); authored anchors = outskirts bridge, The Embassy dome, bare buildings. Procedural fill seeded city blocks, dark alleys.
- Materials/lighting: concrete, glass, steel; moonless night; red emissive window panels and red fog; occasional red lightning in rain.
- Audio: low hum, rain, distant machinery; hallucinated whispers only when unmasked.
- Mechanic: contaminant exposure meter that increases hallucination (audio/visual distortion, false "humans"); a mask item reduces it (source: masks "mitigate" the effects).
- Entity: Servants shadow the player at distance in groups, herd to dead ends/enclosed spaces; capture on opportunity. Avoid combat gameplay beyond last-resort.
- Exit: Outskirts bridges over water to Level 11 (guarded). Vortex is never seen directly, so do not render it as a destination.
- Verticality: modest (towers exist); interiors bare.
- ABSENT: real humans, working water, loot caches, any claimed artifacts.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add friendly humans or NPC survivors.
- Do not make Servants immediately hostile ("not immediately hostile").
- Do not show the Vortex directly or make it an enterable exit.
- Do not give interior furnishings; buildings are "bare and gutted".
- Do not state a fixed map size.
