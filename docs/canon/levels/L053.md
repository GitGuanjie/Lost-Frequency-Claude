## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a normal house with all exits looping back, a never-dark morning sky, random basement food pile, passive faceless visitors, notes through the mail slot.
- Layout: single authored suburban house (bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, living room, TV, basement with concrete floor and food pile). No procedural streaming.
- Windows: show a bright neighbourhood with distant people; front door and any window break teleport the player back inside (loop exit).
- Mechanic: seeded random basement food restock; carry food to fridge; water only at the fridge; clock stuck near early morning feel.
- Mail slot: player can post notes; nothing returns. Optional end state: player carrying a note ends the run (not stated, avoid).
- Exit: the only sourced external exit is the red-painted wooden door to Level 9 (locked one side) and external rescue; implement as interact with locked door or a scripted rescue ending.
- Entities: faceless tall visitors, passive, seated or at windows; no attacks.
- Audio/lighting: not stated beyond early-morning daylight; keep soft room tone.
- Verticality: house floors and basement only.
- ABSENT: combat, hostile entities, procedural rooms, vehicles.
