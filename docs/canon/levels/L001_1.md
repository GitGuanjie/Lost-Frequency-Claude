## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a single corridor degrades through five sectors from stable to broken.
- Layout grammar: authored cross-section (white cinder block, blue tile) extruded along one axis; sector parameters (ceiling 7-12 ft to ~20 ft, width ~12 to 6-20 ft) drive seeded chunks. Outposts as authored anchors at sector junctions/recesses (Outpost 1 and 2 Sector 1/2 junction, 3 shelf, Jakob's Hut, 4 crate).
- Sector 1: fluorescent hum, crates. Sector 2: tilted floor ramps, hue shifts, repeated segments, pipes blocking, rare wall noclip triggers. Sector 3: near-dark, unsourced bright patches, antiseptic note. Sector 4: monochrome, wobbling geometry, partial no-collision, void below.
- Crate supply density falls with distance (simple inventory flags).
- Exits: backtrack to entrance (walk_in trigger); seeded noclip triggers in Sectors 2-3 to Level 1; Sector 4 noclip to void with no return.
- Entity policy: none in Sector 1; Smiler/Hound/Skin-Stealer sparse in Sector 2; Smiler common in Sector 3 (dark); Sector 4 floating glitched entities that hover.
- Verticality: slopes in Sector 2 only; Sector 4 wobble/void. No towers.
- ABSENT: any confirmed Sector 5 content, other-level exits.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Sector 5 a reachable level; page says unconfirmed and "Likely Inaccessible".
- Do not give Sector 1 dangerous entities.
- Do not let Sector 4 noclip return to Level 1.
- Do not add exits to Levels 2/4 (only mentioned in unrelated anecdote) or Level 1.2 (link only).
- Do not make flashlights fix Sector 3 darkness.
