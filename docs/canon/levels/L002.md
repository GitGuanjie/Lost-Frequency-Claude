## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: 45-degree maze of cramped, chalky machine-lined tunnels; series-wired lights; locked doors with destination-coded colours; infinite void rooms; post-blackout whirring half-dead machinery.
- Layout: seeded chunks of tunnels on a 45-degree grid, segment lengths multiples of 5 m, width often shoulder-tight; authored anchors: Office Space EL3A with defunct tram tracks, signposts, a Macchina blade door.
- Materials: chalky concrete and brick, pipes/machines, flatbeds, barrels; doors in colour/material codes per exit table.
- Lighting: sparse fluorescents white/orange, wired in series: breaking one light blacks out a 3x3 to 6x6 grid; optional violet emergency lights in brownout parts.
- Audio: fluorescent buzz, machine whirring; quiet; reverb of narrow corridor.
- Stateful: light-grid blackout; chalk powder on contact; void room as unlit zero-size-limit arena with only the doorway as light.
- Exits: door colours (blue to 175, pink to 997), engraved "70", rainbow to 283, plywood, metal, elevator-like doors, etc., as ExitTriggers.
- Entities: sourced only; keep Clumps ambush at corners, Smilers in dark, Hounds packs; others optional.
- Verticality: none stated (stairs not described).
- ABSENT: active major group base, abundant food or medicine, skin-stealers.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not use right-angle-only corridors; turns are 45 degrees.
- Do not make machines functional or coordinated.
- Do not add food or medical caches.
- Do not place Skin-Stealers.
- Do not stock the void room with anything but a doorway; it is lightless and soundless.
- Do not treat the Macchina or Aiding Stragglers as alive.
- Do not invent a Level 2.2 layout.
