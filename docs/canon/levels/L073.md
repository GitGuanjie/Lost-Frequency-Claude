## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: arrive near a safe castle across a hostile dark red island; run, do not look, do not use light.
- Layout: one finite island (authored): blood-red black ground, ocean ring, castle on the highest point with walls, gatehouse, stone path. Procedural fill only for ground scatter.
- Lighting: fixed full moon, occasional cloud-cover blackouts; player light disabled (flashlight policy: forbidden in the open, per "DO NOT use an artificial light source"). Castle interior lit and furnished.
- Audio: wind, distant hostile sounds heard but not seen; castle fireplace and room tone.
- Mechanic: sprint to the castle gate; stopping or looking around raises hordes; banging on the door ends the run. Looking outside castle windows is blocked (boarded).
- Castle: three floors, attic closed; infinite food/water table; 37 children (names not given).
- Exits: none; a game exit must be flagged as non-canon or omitted.
- Entities: sourced entities appear only as unseen presence (sound, silhouette), never rendered in detail ("YOU DO NOT WANT TO SEE"). CORAN is castle-bound, non-hostile to humans.
- Verticality: castle three floors plus attic; island highest point.
- ABSENT: any exit, safe zones outside the castle, light sources outside.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add a working exit or quick escape; none is known.
- Do not add safe areas outside the Silver Castle.
- Do not allow flashlights outside; do not show entities in full.
- Do not make CORAN hostile to the player.
- Do not add a day/night cycle.
- Do not claim the "Level 6" origin is confirmed.
