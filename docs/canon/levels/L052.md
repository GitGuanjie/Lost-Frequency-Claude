## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: single limestone cavern entered through a shallow crawl passage; entrance mouth is smaller from inside -> authored cavern with a tight crawl-in tunnel (crouch-only) that cannot be backtracked; seeded stalactites/rock fill with varying ceiling height (some 2 m).
- No natural light: darkness with a mandatory flashlight (per-level policy: flashlight required, battery/light cue); drips as the only audio plus muffled room tone; reverb large cave.
- Pickups/notes: three remains at the entrance (bloat, active decay, skeleton) as set dressing; Dina A.'s notebook as an examine note with text degrading over entries (testimony); reddish-brown drawing decals (campfire figures, animals, hand prints, eye pairs); one extinguished campfire and crude tools.
- Stateful mechanic: sense of being observed grows with time in the cave (subtle eye-pair decals multiplying, look-at triggers) with no visible entity; food and water sparse (bugs, ceiling drips).
- Exits: none stated. Return via the entrance is impossible. Provide no ExitTrigger; the session ends only by player choice or hidden unspecified means flagged as unknown.
- Verticality: none beyond ceiling height variation. Scale: length unknown, so use a long streamed cave rather than a fixed small room.
