## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: concussive lightning strikes at unpredictable moments; interiors are safe; extraction is only by deliberately entering a discharge.
- Layout: authored concrete tower cluster plus seeded chunk streaming of brutalist blocks (concrete material, grayscale); interiors enclosed rooms; one spired tower with repurposed rooms (outpost ruin).
- Lighting: no stars, unlit void sky; pulsing floodlights on tower exoskeletons from the dynamic light pool; monochrome fog.
- Audio: thunder with delay; hearing-loss effect (low-pass/ringing) after strikes; pressure/sternum thud. No wind or crickets claimed.
- Stateful mechanic: seeded strike scheduler (unpredictable interval); outdoors strikes kill/stun by exposure rules; indoors safe. Strike warning is not stated, so keep it unannounced or only a brief flash.
- Exit: a rare discharge-contact trigger to Level 3 (noclip type, probabilistic, lethal-feeling).
- Entrance to this level is handled in the source levels.
- Verticality: skyscrapers with interior rooms and a spired tower; no ladder or elevator described.
- Entities: Windows in reflective glass only, outdoors-facing; optional.
- ABSENT: settlements, living NPCs, safe outdoor zones, 3.5 km cap as hard wall (it is just the furthest documented traversal).
