## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: whole level is a plastic scale model; Moon surface covered in ~30 m grey lead paint at standard gravity -> large open grey-plastic lunar terrain using rock/concrete-like grey shader with plastic sheen; standard walk gravity; no low-g.
- Authored anchors: PlasticWorks compound with main gate (exit), a ruined Bluehole shaft site 1 km away (headframe, caved shaft, blue-dyed plastic), mining columns; chunk-stream grey craters/ridges.
- SOURCE: two weather states tied to Beauford -> stateful weather director: toxic lemon mist (damage-over-time, mask helps) or sandstorm (visibility/fog, dust-mite allergy discomfort), triggered when Beauford 'approaches'.
- Beauford: only as distant colossal silhouette/shadow and felt low-frequency vocalisation; never reachable or fought; may cause a 'moonquake' camera shake.
- Moon Mites: giant harmless agents that flee from the player after sandstorms; eggs as optional food flag.
- Exit: walk to the PlasticWorks gate; ExitTrigger destination not stated, so use a configurable placeholder gateway and mark it in-game as 'unspecified'.
- Entrance: Level 57 Moon painting feeds the player in; B.N.T.G. entrances secret.
- Verticality: mine shaft/lift may exist at PlasticWorks (mining columns) but depth is unstated; keep shallow. Lighting/audio unspecified: use sunless black sky, hard light, wind/dust, mite scuttle; keep 'Victorian study' unseen.
