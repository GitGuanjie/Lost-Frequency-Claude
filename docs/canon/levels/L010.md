## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: calm infinite farmland, a thin 1 m soil crust over a worm mass, and unused vehicle tracks.
- Layout grammar: seeded chunk streaming of wheat/barley plots separated by constant-height hedge lines; authored anchors are barns/sheds, a lake, and a dirt-road corridor with two ruts and grass strip.
- Materials/lighting/audio: grass, soil, wood, water; flat overcast light, low fog, occasional light rain; wind through crops, no day-night cycle.
- Stateful mechanic: digging is only meaningful as a hazard; a hole deeper than 1 m triggers a worm surge (slip, damage). Keep digging optional and rare.
- Exits: long road (distance-based or seeded trigger) to L11; hidden canola patch to L184; lake swim to an unspecified aquatic level (leave as UNKNOWN or fade).
- Verticality: none (flat plain; buildings single-storey; the soil depth is 1 m).
- Entity policy: only the worms; nothing roams the surface.
- ABSENT: vehicles, settlements, towers, dedicated outposts, combat.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not spawn tractors or cars; tracks exist but no vehicles have been seen.
- Do not make the tracks regrow or allow crops on them.
- Do not add a day-night cycle or sun path; the sky stays overcast.
- Do not add hostile surface entities; class says 0/5.
- Do not make the narrator's parental-death story a mandatory quest; it is testimony.
