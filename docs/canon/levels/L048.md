## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: endless warm beach at permanent sunset with luxury hotels and a tropical forest, many multi-route portals.
- Layout grammar: sand strip with authored hotel/house clusters, ocean at one edge; forest belt 200 m inland (chunk-streamed palms and mixed trees); optional farmhouses and trench lines in the forest.
- Materials/lighting/audio: sand, modern concrete/ceramic interiors, palm foliage; low red-yellow fixed sun, warm light, lukewarm shallow surf, birds (qarrot calls).
- Stateful mechanic: portal variety rather than combat; lake swim and hotel noclip as key exits.
- Exits: hotel noclip (L581), lake floor swim (L121), trench (L49), farmhouse (L10), play-dead (L100, low seeded chance, flagged unconfirmed).
- Verticality: hotel stories only; no tall landmarks beyond.
- Entity policy: sourced docile fauna only; The Shadow stays rumoured.
- ABSENT: hostile entities, day-night cycle, storms.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add a day-night cycle or noon sun; it is permanent sunset at 30% noon light.
- Do not make fauna hostile; all are passive/non-hostile.
- Do not treat play-dead to Level 100 as a guaranteed exit.
- Do not assign a survival class; placeholders are unresolved.
- Do not place unique species near the beach; they are ~30 km north.
