## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: vaporwave desert with a sea and a neon city; calming music causes permanent Tune-Out; exits are via TVs and hyperlinks.
- Layout grammar: one long axis: ocean/coast (warm) - Cool Desert with authored purple marble ruins and oases - Neon City (cold). Mountains as barrier walls on the sides; sky grid shader.
- Materials/lighting/audio: yellow/violet sand, purple marble, neon, glass; pink-orange horizontal-striped sun, deep-purple sky over city; synthwave-like lounge/smooth-jazz music loop (differs per player); surf, car hum, arcade noise.
- Stateful mechanic: Tune-Out meter that rises while standing still listening; an alarm-like sound resets it; at max the player becomes a Static Ghost (run ends).
- Exits: TVs in the city with a channel dial (level shown varies per TV); Trench windows (safe vs broken list); arcade cabinet; broken links go to a white void.
- Verticality: city towers and 2-storey malls; trenches down to 500 m in the sea.
- Entity policy: Static Ghosts (harmless, ignoring), Binary Fish, Light Traffic, Windows only.
- ABSENT: Partygoers, mouse-compatible terminals, working compass.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Static Ghosts hostile; the page calls them harmless.
- Do not allow swimming; the sea is nearly intangible, you walk/breathe in it.
- Do not add Partygoers; the page says none have been seen.
- Do not stock the broken links with working destinations; they go to the Null.
- Do not make the compass work or add a moving sun.
