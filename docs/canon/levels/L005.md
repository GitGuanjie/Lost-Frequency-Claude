## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: infinite 1930s hotel with three connected zones and "floor" elevators that only relocate within the level up to floor 12.
- Layout grammar: authored anchors = Main Hall corridor set, Beverly Room (round-ish ballroom, central table, chandelier, many doors), Boiler Room (pipes, boilers). Procedural fill = seeded corridors of numbered doors; room-number placards with no pattern.
- Materials: wood, marble, carpet, ornate wallpaper (red/gold), brass pipes in boiler zone; warm dim lights, some "candle" fixtures that buzz.
- Audio: quiet random-track jazz (synthesised pad/music box), distant party chatter through walls, boiler machinery, dripping.
- Stateful mechanic: elevator panel with floors 1-382; floors 1-12 teleport within Level 5 (same floor), 13+ is death/disappearance (seeded). Door-to-area graph: Beverly Room doors -> Main Hall (high probability) or Boiler Room.
- Exits: door/elevator/arcade ExitTriggers per table; flicker-in-unison event spawns a door to Level 427; Boiler Room deep walk to Level 6 with heat damage.
- Verticality: none beyond elevator illusion (floors 1-12 are same-floor); floors above 12 are not rendered.
- Entity policy: Deathmoth swarms from dark rooms are the only sourced active threat; others stay absent or ambient reports only.
- ABSENT: no friendly combat; no Lobby Room geometry (memory-erased rumor only).
