## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE FACT: a childhood bedroom (9-12 years) with a doorway onto an infinite black void -> compact authored bedroom (beige drywall, carpet, wooden door, desk, closet as in the interview TESTIMONY, but with the sourced fixed items: single bed with blue floral sheet) built from plaster/carpet/wood materials; seeded variant per run.
- SOURCE FACT: dim sterile white light that must stay on -> a single light switch interact; turning it off releases Smilers from under-bed/dark spaces (sourced entity behaviour).
- SOURCE FACT: void with lowered gravity, no upward swimming, rope of ~137 m -> unlit void volume behind the door with slow downward drift, breathable; a rope anchored at the doorknob lets the player climb back. Fall past the rope = fail state (dehydration).
- SOURCE FACT: stress mould, 8 hours -> mould patch decals that grow with play-time and a stress meter effect (blurred vision/heartbeat) escalating after ~8 in-game hours.
- Appliances inert; devices brought in work; bed bugs bites on lying down.
- Exits: sleep on the bed -> Level 9 street (ExitTrigger sleep); the phrase 'I'm so bored...' as an interact/emote -> Level 25.
- Verticality: the void descends endlessly; room is single storey. Entities: Smilers, mould, windows only; no combat.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add communities or other players.
- Do not make the void a barrier wall; it is enterable.
- Do not let batteries power the room's appliances.
- Do not give a chase/entity in the void; the page says it appears devoid.
- Do not treat interview details (pink stool, Barbie poster) as canonical room contents: they are one wanderer's testimony.
- Do not add windows to every room: only if the childhood room had them.
