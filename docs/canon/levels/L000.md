## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT -> IMPLEMENTATION
- Peripheral Shift (layout rewrites when unobserved) -> seeded chunk streaming; chunks behind the player regenerate with a new seed when out of view for N seconds (some "stable" arch zones exempt).
- Layout grammar: yellow-wallpaper rooms/halls/stairs as procedural fill; authored variant anchors: arch rooms (stable, deep carpet), pillar lattice halls, hole grids (kill/respawn volume, no survivors), blackout zones (no light, ankle-deep fluid, silence), red rooms (closed loop).
- Materials: wallpaper, damp carpet (shallower in pillar rooms), fluorescent emissive panels; fog sparse.
- Audio: ballast hum with random loudness spikes; rare whispers/scratching; silence inside blackout zones.
- Isolation Effect: no NPCs, no other travellers; notes do nothing to help.
- Exit: flickering wall segment as rare interactable/noclip trigger to Level 1, seeded low probability.
- Verticality: none beyond stairs ("stairs" mentioned) and pits as downward kill volumes.
- Entities: none spawned; at most unexplained dark-figure glimpses as non-interactive ambience, labelled REPORTED.
- ABSENT: combat, collectibles beyond optional Almond Water, any safe base.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add confirmed entities; page says existence unknown.
- Do not make holes a route to another level; no survivors reported.
- Do not make red rooms escapable by puzzle; "extremely difficult, if not impossible".
- Do not let the player meet companions.
- Do not put Base Alpha or the Manila Room content in; only link targets.
