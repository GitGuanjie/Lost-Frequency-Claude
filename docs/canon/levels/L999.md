## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE FACT: floating tropical island in a starry void, greyscale, 13-16 C -> unlit starry void environment (emissive stars), a flat greyscale island with fine smoke-grey beach sand, grey palm trees, rocks; desaturation via a global grading shader (colour stripped on this level; held-items colour dims not required).
- SOURCE FACT: sand and rocks floating beyond the perimeter -> static particle/mesh props hovering beyond the island edge; the player cannot walk off but may peer into the void (clamp edge or invisible wall; the page gives no void-entry rule).
- SOURCE FACT: flat forest interior, motionless trees despite wind sound -> streamed tree field (no sway), wind audio loop without foliage motion; coarse dirt with no grass; constant brightness aura (no day/night, fixed light).
- SOURCE FACT: dormant houses near the epicenter with no paths -> a few authored shells (concrete/wood/glass) with sparse furnishings, empty beds, closed blinds, unconnected by paths.
- Exit: single trapdoor among benches at the epicenter -> ExitTrigger (interact) returning to the echoing halls; entrance is the arrival from those halls.
- Verticality: none (flat). Entity policy: none. Must stay ABSENT: any change over time, collectibles, notes, entities, hills.
