## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Sideways entrance room + gravity flip → small furnished carpeted room with puddle; the door is an interact-triggered "fall" into the ocean (player drops ~4.5 m into a water volume); no re-entry without rope.
- Infinite ocean → very large water volume, dim ambient light, high concrete ceiling slab; distant rock islands seeded in chunks.
- Depth zones → depth-driven fog/light: Daylight dim, Twilight darker/colder ~1 km, Midnight black, Abyss below 7 km with tar/rock mounds and bubbling.
- 30-minute breath → oxygen capacity far larger than normal near surface (sourced).
- Rope/ladder: player ladder from door to surface (item flag).
- Exits: Level 8 cave as dive trigger; Abyss-unconscious as oxygen-depletion/sleep trigger; Level 9 door at 150 m depth. Level 880 only as optional rumoured far-west boat.
- Entities: Thing and Tiny as scripted agents limited to their zones; light vs noise as distractors. Skeletons as decor.
- Verticality: dominant (surface to >7 km).
- ABSENT: other sea life, light sources outside entrance lamp.

## Canon traps (do not)
- No fish or living marine fauna; only skeletons.
- Do not crush the player with pressure; the page says it does not.
- Do not let entities leave their zones.
- Do not add a boat as a certain exit (unproven).
- Do not make cold lethal short-term.
