## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a tiny white room with table, chair and locked door; the exit is a timed ritual; the room extends into a white abyss.
- Layout grammar: one authored bright white room (table, chair, door) with nooks opening into an unlit-white void; scattered old furniture props, some sunk in the floor; copycat doors that loop back.
- Materials: plaster white, wood table/chair, emissive white; void as unlit white.
- Audio: near silence, room tone only (page gives no sound; keep minimal).
- Mechanic: a seat-time counter (about 15 min on the chair), a locked door interaction, copycat door passage, then a 1 hour wait timer and door retry. Implement via ExitTrigger type `condition`/`timed` with scaled real-time or an accelerated debug variant; destination chosen from the listed eight.
- Fog: memory fog effect (UI blur or note-erasing) in the abyss, non-permanent.
- Hole under the table is inaccessible; table immovable.
- Self-censoring: in-game screenshot/recording feature could show SMPTE (optional gimmick).
- Entities: none.
- Verticality: none.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add entities; not even a Window.
- Do not let the main door open without the procedure (an opening on first try is "lucky").
- Do not make the copycat doors exits.
- Do not make the hole under the table accessible.
- Do not invent a Level 13 edge.
- Do not assume the log is the permanent truth: "Revision 1" differed.
