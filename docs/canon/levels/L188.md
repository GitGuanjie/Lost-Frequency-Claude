## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Closed white concrete atrium (45 m wide, 30 m high) with evenly spaced windows, central pentagonal path and birchwood circle, living moss, glass railings, table with gramophone and clock; constant cold, near-silence.
- Arrival through a window that vanishes within 2-3 minutes; after that, no known exit (design a ticking window-closing event).
- Atrium instability: window count, table position, and window layout drift when the player sleeps or looks away (stateful changes recorded in logs).
- Corridor annex: occasional mahogany doors into shifting white corridors with locked doors; blank white rooms.
- Neurological hazard: touching listed objects triggers hallucination effects (audio/visual), recommended counter-play hiding under cover for 6-8 hours (timed hold).
- Entities: window entities (observe), pink dress faceling and skin-stealer only in corridors, shadow silhouettes in peripheral vision.
- Log pickups: audio/text notes from the Owl-Eyes team (logs 1-6) as in-world documents; avoid dwelling on gore.
- Vertical: corridors span several floors with stairwells; walkways above the atrium.
- ABSENT: settlements, safe exit, sunlight, noise.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not build a working exit through the wooden circle or windows; page denies them.
- Do not add a settlement or NPC community.
- Do not make the room noisy; silence is canon.
- Do not present confetti hole as an exit.
- Do not treat "space station / Mercury" memory as a real level (text links it back to /level-188).
- Do not use corrupted Gatsby text as lore facts.
