## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT -> IMPLEMENTATION
- Small town of ~4,000 that "adapts to what you remember" -> compact authored town (main street, breakfast place, school, house) sized as a small blocks grid; no chunk streaming needed.
- Memory-building mechanic: stateful "Remember" interaction at set points (school basement door, house, diner) alters geometry/textures toward a nostalgic version; spawned memory items persist in inventory flags.
- Rot: a decay shader that spreads while the player looks at it ("grows when you notice it") and also over time when away; walls thin, paint chips; Ms. Garcia style set piece (residents are static figures, not combatants).
- Residents: simple static or idle NPC figures (not entities); Joe Vee holding a soup bowl; unsettling ambience.
- Audio: faint cracking/skin sound from the sky (use sparingly), muffled rust-smell not representable; add soft town room tone.
- Exits: none stated. Make the "stay" pressure the ending: leaving is not offered. Do not invent a door.
- Verticality: school basement ("Below, below, below") as a short descent. Entity policy: none; ABSENT: combat, exits to 199/201.
