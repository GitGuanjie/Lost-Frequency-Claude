## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a civilised island cut off by an ocean that kills any vessel; exit is practically impossible.
- Layout grammar: authored island ring (cliff coast, mountain belt, inner basin with farm villages and a central Hyperia hub); ocean as unlit-to-blue water volume beyond a lethal coastline. Chunk-stream farmland between mountain walls.
- Materials/lighting/audio: wood towns, rock, grass, crops; pale pink sky and permanent sun (low-fixed); wind, surf, village ambience, river sounds.
- Stateful mechanic: leaving by boat ends in a Kraken/whirlpool or ship-breaking sea; treat as a failed run with death; successful crossing remains unimplemented (page says "almost impossible").
- Exits: sailing triggers are DANGEROUS+UNKNOWN; a seeded chance of reaching L114/L284 is not supported by the page; prefer no success path.
- Verticality: mountains and hill watchtowers; no towers beyond that.
- Entity policy: Kraken only offshore; ocean entities as unnamed shapes; no land fauna.
- ABSENT: hostile land creatures, any boat-safe sea.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the ocean passable by ordinary boats; it breaks even the strongest vessels.
- Do not put the Kraken near the island; it sleeps far away.
- Do not declare success for the sea exit; destinations are only "theoretical".
- Do not fill the island with hostile entities; none are documented on land.
- Do not use the 5/5 entity rating as a reason to invent named monsters.
