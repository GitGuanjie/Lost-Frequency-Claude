## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a harmless, nostalgic, sand-floored complex of painted rooms with soothing rain sound and toys.
- Layout: procedural room/corridor grid (seeded) with sand-covered floors (sand material, slight drifts); some rooms sand-free with a door or a window as the exit.
- Materials: cracked/peeling blue, cream, red paint (red rarest) over plaster or corroded steel; sand; no-function ceiling light fixtures; light beams (emissive shafts) from random ceiling/wall spots, sunlight-like glow.
- Audio: constant soft rain (no source), dry quiet air; sand-footstep surface.
- Stateful mechanic: persistent footprints and marks in sand (no wind, no water); rooms regenerate only after decades (do not regenerate in-session). Toys embedded in sand are optional interactable notes.
- Entrance rule: a seeded small-probability diversion from other level exits, scaled by player age if modelled (avoid unless needed).
- Exits: a door or window in a sand-free room restores the intended destination.
- Verticality: none; ceilings are impenetrable voidstone.
- Entities: none. ABSENT: bases, NPCs, food, water, hostile entities, hazards.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add any threat, entity, or survival pressure; survival class is 0.
- Do not add food, water, electricity or functioning lights.
- Do not add bases or communities.
- Do not let the player break through the ceiling; it is voidstone.
- Do not treat the Level 40 comment as the page's statement.
- Do not make rooms regenerate within a session.
