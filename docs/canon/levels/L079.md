## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: seven rooms off one corridor, ~370 m2, mid-century medical research clinic -> compact fully authored interior (no streaming): main corridor with doors alternating R/L, room 7 at the end; clinical plaster/tile/linoleum, wood, metal; fire damage in the office.
- Room set dressing exactly as listed: chalkboard note, equipment list, examination room with locked cabinet items, X-ray with child's arm film, 6 beds/6 dressers items, operating theatre with fluid slurry and metal bits, machine with 16 clocks showing different times.
- Stateful mechanic: the machine's ~30-minute cycle (knock, tremor, no-clip sensation) and the level's 'temporal instability': a visible in-level clock (16 different times) and a displacement effect on prolonged stay; exit door arrival 'inconsistent' (random time offset), shown in a log UI like the submission log.
- Audio: electrically distorted sobbing from the machine; otherwise sparse room tone; reverb small hard rooms. Lighting not stated: keep dim and rely on flashlight policy.
- Exit: the single corridor-start door; ExitTrigger destination 'any level', chosen with configurable seeded randomisation and flagged as 'not stated'; no other exits.
- Verticality: none. Entities: none; no NPCs; items (Seer powder, Almond Water, pills) as examine/pickup flags only.
