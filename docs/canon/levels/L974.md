## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a closed pink house tailored to the visitor; the only stalker is Kitty; exit is gifting Kitty a cute item; bed plus DreamSleep leads to 974.1.
- Layout: one authored ~970 sq ft house (bedroom, master bedroom, two baths, living room, kitchen/dining); no exterior door; windows textured with a flat coquette scene.
- Materials: Baker Miller pink plaster walls, pastel furniture, wood banquette, emissive-free warm lamps; light levels not stated.
- Audio: soft lullaby-style pad (music-box fits the "foggy lullaby" caption); room tone.
- Stateful: sanity drift while staying; food quality decays toward sugar; food respawns; per-run seeded variation (utensils, frames, posters).
- Exit: handing Kitty an item flagged cute and pre-owned triggers fade-to-sleep and load of a generic safe level; bed/computer path to 974.1 (sub-level, separate dossier).
- Kitty: scripted stalker, never attacks; goes away and food vanishes with aggression or damage; child-flag variant nurtures.
- Verticality: none.
- ABSENT: bases, hostile combat, windows to a real outside, Owner unless 974.1 triggers it.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Kitty hostile or killing.
- Do not add an exterior door or real outdoors.
- Do not fill the empty rooms with furniture (only dining room and bedroom).
- Do not allow sleep-to-974.1 anywhere except the bed.
- Do not invent the email text (missing from snapshot).
- Do not treat 973/975 links as exits (nav only).
