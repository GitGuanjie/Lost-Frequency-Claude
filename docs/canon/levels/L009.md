## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: endless midnight suburb; powerless furnished houses; dangerous streets with fog that spawns The Mangled; many branching exits.
- Layout grammar: seeded chunk streaming of street grid, house plots, sidewalks, grass edges. Authored anchors: walkway-to-field edge, airport, playground, arrow signs.
- Materials/lighting: wet asphalt, stone sidewalk, plaster/wood houses, dead trees; near-black ambience, mostly dead street lamps with occasional flicker; no powered interior lights; flashlight is the main light.
- Audio: low wind, faint room tone, wet footsteps; dread bed, no music.
- Stateful mechanic: fog patches (seeded) mark where The Mangled can spawn; houses may be empty or furnished; entering houses has a random chance of teleport to Level 53.
- Exits: arrow-sign trail (long walk) to Level 11 via airport variant; grass-field boundary triggers to 9.1/10; no-clip floor to 60; house door random roll to 53; powerlines roll to 113; playground tube to 283.
- Entity policy: only sourced entities; The Mangled tied to fog. Keep the rest ambient/rare.
- Verticality: none (flat suburb, 1-2 storey houses).
- ABSENT: working electricity, bases/communities.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make appliances or lamps functional.
- Do not add a community base ("none have been recorded").
- Do not spawn The Mangled without fog.
- Do not treat the forum post as the level's rules (it is testimony, and the post's author claimed the level "doesn't exist").
