## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE FACT: planetoid of tree-tunnel caverns, no day-night, little light on dense surface -> a surface forest layer (dense trunks, dark canopy fog, constant faint light, no day cycle) and subsurface glow-room caverns, via seeded chunk streaming with bark/wood and moss materials.
- SOURCE FACT: Sorries light the caverns -> colourful moving point lights in a light pool with a looped whisper-'sorry' audio bed; damp cool air, cavern reverb.
- SOURCE FACT: ruins with swapped shapes (spherical pyramid, square Colosseum, triangular Parthenon, pyramidal lighthouse) -> authored anchors in glow rooms; Latin examine notes and the plaque.
- SOURCE FACT: buried sentries triggered by a phrase -> a spoken-phrase mechanic is hard in-engine; implement as a key/choice interaction note or a read-aloud prompt that triggers a loud scream with a hearing-damage effect.
- SOURCE FACT: hollow core with Green Giants and Heart of Water -> large vertical hall with giant trunks (2 miles tall is the largest; use a scaled trunk) and a water volume; entering it triggers a random noclip exit (7, 121, or 43).
- Base Seedling as an authored safe outpost with the Level 135 hollow-tree gate. Entrances are four hollow trees.
- Verticality: strongly sourced (surface forest, caverns, hollow core). Entities: only sourced ones, avoid inventing combat.
