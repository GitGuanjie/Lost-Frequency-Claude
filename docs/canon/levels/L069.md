## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Infinite highway between concrete walls → seeded endless asphalt lane with side concrete slabs, almost no props; fog shader with tight visibility and darkness.
- The car is the only safe zone → a drivable car prop (vehicle controller with no fuel, rare crashes lethal) with a clear fog radius around it; exiting triggers the Beings From Above.
- Beings From Above → spider-leg appendages descending from darkness as a timer-based threat after leaving the car; grab leads to death (no kill option).
- Fragment compartment under the car as an interact item; removing it drops safe zone.
- Whispers: shared text/audio overlay appearing at intervals.
- Exits: tunnel found by driving (seeded, rare), 17 h compressed as a timed drive with fog fading and lights on; roadside wall noclip spots to Level 22.
- Verticality: none. Absent: bases, other cars, props, any light except the car's and yours.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add other vehicles or buildings; "devoid of vehicles".
- Do not give fuel mechanics.
- Do not allow killing the Beings From Above.
- Do not make leaving the car safe or give the player ambient light.
- Do not treat the Level 0 and Level 4 exits as confirmed.
