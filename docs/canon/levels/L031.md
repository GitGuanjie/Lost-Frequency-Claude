## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE: infinitely looping rink, arcade east, food court, rental, Cinema north -> small hub of authored wings (rink loop with wooden floor, arcade, food court, rental, cinema) connected by wrap-around teleport seams; 'infinite loop' realised by seamless wrap, not by large size.
- Materials: smooth wood rink, rough black carpet outside the gate, blue-wall rental, arcade carpet, emissive colour fluorescents, star fixture (rotating); mostly dimmed ceiling. Audio: muffled radio pop (synth/chiptune), skates on wood, laughter, arcade bleeps, pizza smell is text-only.
- Defining stateful mechanic: a 'comfort/stay' counter. The longer the player stays, familiarity decays (music repeats, NPC dialogue loops, apathy UI/desaturation) while the Coach increasingly approaches and offers exits; the diary shows this as lore notes.
- Facelings: docile employee agents (food court, DJ, wanderers); Coach as a friendly guide with exit offer; no combat.
- Exits: arcade noclip into a working machine (L40); hidden white door '70' in the back arcade area; 'Employees only!' door (L232); Coach-led exit with unspecified destination (use configurable placeholder).
- Interactables: green card dispenser, free arcade, free food and drinks; litter rule causes the clerk to refuse free food.
- Verticality: none (single floor; cinema tiers are small stepped benches).
- Absent: harmful entities, permanent communities.
