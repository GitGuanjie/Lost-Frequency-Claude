## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Craterwood: seeded chunk forest, trees growing larger toward the centre (tree scale vs distance to crater), fissure-lined flooded areas (water volumes at fissure edges), day 12 h / night 18 h cycle, slow centimetre-scale floor heave tied to mist pulses.
- Mist is a stateful hazard: exposure meter; filter/mask item reduces it; effects irreversible (permanent debuff flag).
- Hauntwood: observation-collapsed layout, re-seeded when looked away from, but its zone position fixed; moving straight eventually exits.
- Safe base New Hereth: authored partially underground compound with filtered air, plus a small intact church.
- Trail guides: teal toward base, purple toward exits.
- Entities: Dendroids (flee from fire), Grimshells in water, Anthosia pollen patches, Cass as a slow giant moss field; Black Goat as rare lure; none in Hauntwood except rarely.
- Verticality: fissures to about 300 m deep and 200 m trees; keep fissures as no-go drops.
- Absent: other factions inside New Hereth beyond the three named groups.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Hauntwood a high-density monster zone; encounters are low.
- Do not let the mist cure or be reversible.
- Do not invent exit destinations for purple guides.
- Do not treat the diary as article fact; it is testimony from a child.
- Do not eat or drink from the level safely.
