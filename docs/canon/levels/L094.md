## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Day/night cycle (the only stated timing) → day: safe stop-motion town, cartoony siren music; night: music stops, Animations hunt. Grain/low-frame-rate shader (stepped animation) over the whole scene.
- Town authored around a central fountain; houses with furniture; 1930s cars; milk vans.
- Infinite hills: seeded chunks of grass with sparse cars, a water tower, and wall-plus-furniture fragments.
- Floating castle: separate authored interior, fun-house rooms, animation studio with miniature town; photo cartoon effect off.
- Exit triggers: Animated King trial (condition, burst of light, random destination); some houses (door, seeded) to 7, 9, 53.
- Entity policy: Animations at night plus castle Robo-Men; other night entities only as sourced list.
- Verticality: castle floats above hills (reached by method not stated).
- ABSENT: any text on how to reach the castle; do not invent a route.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add night entities during the day.
- Do not add entities in the castle other than Robo-Men and the King.
- Do not state a castle access route; the page is silent.
- Do not make the town's music constant.
- Do not pick which houses lead where; page says only "some houses".
