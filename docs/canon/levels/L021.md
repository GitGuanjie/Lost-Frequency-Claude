## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: four 26-mile hallways, constant repeating doors, labelled destinations, shifting corridors.
- Layout: central square (desk, chairs, safe from entities) with four radial seeded-chunk hallways; rooms stay generic (no wall material stated; do not claim wallpaper).
- Doors: seeded labels per door; implement the table above as ExitTriggers; unlabeled doors go to 1.5; other doors give seeded random outcomes, mostly re-spawn in Level 21 or a blocked wall.
- Shifting: seeded re-roll of hallway segments, occasional blocked passages.
- Exit door: one authored neon red "Exit" sign door right of the desk hallway leading to a non-shifting dark three-hallway zone with more items and organised entities.
- Items: Almond Water, Level Keys, Firesalt as sparse pickups.
- Entities: Clickers (common, hallways only), never in centre square. Lighting and sound not stated.
- Verticality: none stated.
- ABSENT: bases, friendly NPCs, entities in the centre.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add a community or outpost.
- Do not allow entities in the central square.
- Do not make the Exit door safe or in the left hallway; it is right of the desk.
- Do not invent door labels beyond those listed; 20 and 22 appear only as link targets.
- Do not make doors reliable: most lead back to Level 21 or a wall.
