## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: calm endless shallow sea with stilt houses, drifting boats, Deep Eye holes, giant house-wearing Seawalkers.
- Layout: seeded chunk streaming of open water with scattered stilt-house clusters (villages around 10 structures, bridges); authored anchors: Grand Colony, one Deep Eye, snow-roof house, Leilah.
- Materials: water (calm), wood, metal, concrete stilts, sand/rock seabed at 20 m, coral; bright sun, no day/night (source: constant time of day).
- Audio: wind, gentle water, gulls; boat wake only.
- Mechanics: swim with buoyancy and oxygen; boat vehicle pickup; Deep Eye dive exit to Level 8; snow-roof door to 7.7; compass failure optional UI effect (journal TESTIMONY).
- Verticality: 20 m seabed, holes descending to deep dark (up to 12,000 m, treat as abyss fade); houses a few storeys.
- Entities: Seawalkers only, as slow non-aggressive wandering silhouettes, never attacking; gulls as ambient life.
- ABSENT: hostile sea monsters, combat, anything else not in the text.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Seawalkers hostile.
- Do not add a day/night cycle or storms; constant time and calm sea.
- Do not treat the 1705 Captain's Journal as the article's voice; it is in-universe fiction.
- Do not give a Level 7.8 to Level 7 exit; none is stated.
- Do not add waves; the page says minimal waves, only wake.
