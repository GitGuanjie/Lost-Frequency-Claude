## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: non-Euclidean concrete halls with crates, interrupted by random total-blackout Flickering; safe Euclidean corridors; sectors; settlements.
- Layout: seeded chunk streaming of halls (pillar grid, pipes, discoloured walls) with authored anchors: Base Alpha (Aquila), corridor-entrance doors with green exit signs, one corridor maze, crate shelves (Gild). Hall distance distortion via visual-only perceived-depth tricks; corridors laid out Euclidean.
- Materials: concrete, pillars, pipes, puddles, wooden crates, fluorescent panels, green exit light.
- Stateful mechanic: Flickering timer (random, unpredictable); lights go fully off, entities spawn only in halls; player light use in halls during Flickering raises danger; corridors rarely have entities.
- Crates: spawn behind the player or after Flickering, vanish when unobserved; contents tables with hazards.
- Exits: doors keyed to listed destinations (glass, bunker, wooden, plain-white), smooth transition to Level 2 by walking toward pipes and dials, ceiling-hole climb (19), vent (800), spoken string (352), no-clip points. Gate each by its source condition.
- Verticality: none beyond ceiling holes, ventilation ducts, stairs.
- Entities: only the sourced list, hall-only and mostly Flickering-time; Garden Sector as a timed plant transformation.
- ABSENT: day/night, outdoors, working natural locks that persist.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the halls Euclidean or safe at night; Flickering is the core danger.
- Do not populate corridors with entities (rare).
- Do not let crates, locks or paintings persist when unobserved.
- Do not present the first-person story as article fact.
- Do not use level numbers from the entrance list as guaranteed doors in-game; they are conditional/specific.
- Do not treat Garden Sector or Concrete Garden as safe.
- Do not make the "level 15" footnote marker an exit to Level 15; the link target is Level 352.
