## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: a warm pastoral sunflower field with bees, honey, a day-night cycle, and a flower-dust path leading to an endless wheat field where lost people are found.
- Layout: authored camp (lobby with honey bottles, backyard garden), sunflower field, weed field to its left, beehive, tiny tree with weird flower, blue flower; streamed grass chunks to the horizon; wheat field to the south.
- Mechanic: peel a petal from the blue flower, dust drifts, flowers bloom into a path (seeded visual path) leading south toward the wheat field and ending abruptly.
- Day-night: sunrise ~5, sunset ~6 pm in-game clock; stars at night.
- Audio: bees buzzing, wind, soft grass; one rare distant giggle; no combat sounds.
- Items: honey jars (flavour only; no stat claims beyond taste).
- Exits: none sourced; either leave the level without an exit trigger or implement a non-level end (finding Gina) as a narrative close. Do not invent a destination level.
- Verticality: none (gentle hill implied by "sunflower hill" in the author comment).
- ABSENT: hostile entities, combat, danger, tall structures.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add Level 139/141 exits (nav only).
- Do not add hostile entities or horror.
- Do not make the level dark or foggy by default; it has sunsets and stars.
- Do not turn the "human-made" buzz into a monster.
- Do not claim the author's out-of-universe notes are lore (the closing author note mentions "grannies" and "sunflower hill").
