## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Infinite dark forest, crescent moon, no stars → seeded chunk streaming of tree-and-leaf-litter terrain, unlit void sky with a crescent moon disc, dark fog; flashlight allowed (no restriction stated).
- Hanging skeletons clatter in wind → wind synth plus bone-rattle layers and whistle through skull props; optional faint whispers (page frames speech as uncertain).
- Belle lure: a distant orange-dress figure appears, retreats; following leads to the Queen's clearing (authored arena).
- Queen encounter: a stateful respect check (kneel/crouch input, no sprint or attack) decides release (fade, then respawn in an unnamed forest) or a scripted death (trees or ground swallowing). Seeded choice allowed.
- No stable exit before the encounter: no ExitTrigger until the Queen scene resolves.
- Verticality: none (no hills stated). Entity policy: only Belle and Queen; no other creatures.
- Absent: any structures, items, communities, additional monsters.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not add a roaming horde or generic enemies; "only a single entity roams the level".
- Do not give a findable exit; none exists before the Queen.
- Do not make the Queen purely hostile; respect can free you.
- Do not treat the vignette bargain as a standard route.
- Do not add stars to the sky.
