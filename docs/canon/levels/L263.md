## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Endless pink hallway with static windows → seeded or looped corridor of pink wallpaper, slightly darker pink carpet, 17 m pitch-black ceiling, window panels showing animated static noise (emissive shader). Quiet apart from soft static; no lights hum.
- Pink consciousness: any text display shows her messages: put a handheld camera/phone/terminal item; interaction yields short lowercase replies, then optional TTS-style text only. Reactive sadness on leaving.
- Exit: the entry door behind you; turning around always returns to origin. Entrance is a static door near Base Beta (Level 11), referenced externally.
- Revision framing: optional examine notes with draft texts ("please please", emails) for flavour; keep them clearly as drafts/testimony.
- Verticality: none (corridor). Entities: none. Pink is not hostile.
- Absent: monsters, hum, danger meters. A dead past visitor with a backpack and laptop may be an environmental detail (sourced as testimony).

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make Pink hostile or a monster; class 0.
- Do not add other creatures; the earlier "no monsters" draft is Pink's own claim, confirmed by the article's entity line.
- Do not give a separate exit location; the exit is behind you.
- Do not use the old "Untextured" Level 263; it is not in this snapshot.
- Do not make the hallway a maze of rooms.
