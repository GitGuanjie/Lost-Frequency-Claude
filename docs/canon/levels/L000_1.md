## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: damaged spherical-shell space station inside Level 0; uneven damage drives light and temperature.
- Layout: authored hub of corridor/room modules (bedroom, kitchen, gym, greenhouse) with sliding doors; one view window onto the hollow central cavity (SOURCE: "hollowed-out section in what is believed to be the centre"). Procedural fill for damage state per module seed.
- Materials: metal, glass, emissive panels; exposed-wire props in damaged modules. Lighting: bright by default, dark modules by seed.
- Stateful mechanic: per-module climate state (normal / extreme cold / extreme heat); extreme zones damage over time (SOURCE: "unsurvivability"). Show hazard via fog/tint, not text.
- Hallucination layer: occasional audio/visual false cues (SOURCE: "visual/auditory hallucinations"). No other wanderers ever appear.
- Exits: walk-in trigger back to Level 0 at a threshold door; low-probability noclip trigger to Level 1.
- Verticality: gentle only; the Level 0 "stacked floors" view may be a window vista, not playable.
- Entities: none. Automaton props (cleaning/cooking) allowed as non-hostile scripted ambience.
- ABSENT: NPC wanderers, outposts, combat, hostile creatures, named entities.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make it a populated hub or base: the page says it is not viable for outposts.
- Do not make Ria Valpuri a playable guaranteed NPC; she appears only in a narrative vignette.
- Do not treat the sphere shape as confirmed map geometry: "believed" only.
- Do not add Level 0 yellow wallpaper rooms inside the station except as a reference to the parent; the final vignette paragraph describes Level 0 halls, not the station.
- Do not invent a hostile robot class.
