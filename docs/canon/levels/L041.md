## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: boiling tar lake, safe only on islands and piers, Leviathan ambush, four unique landmark exits.
- Layout: circular lake bounded by 50 m dead ring then unreachable ~15 m dunes (visible backdrop only, no collision beyond). Tar slab as lethal volume (contact damage), sand islands as scattered patches, wooden/concrete piers 2-6 m up with burned gaps.
- Materials: asphalt tar shader with bubbles and emissive fire; hot charcoal sand; wood, concrete, metal supports; greying fog; dim light with fire pools.
- Audio: bubbling tar, fire, hiss; heavy breathing under fumes. Light level beyond fog not stated.
- Stateful: fume intoxication meter near tar; hot sand damage when stationary; platform sinking and burning events (seeded); rare spreading fire.
- Entity: Collagen Leviathan scripted only at island edges and piers, leaps and grabs, burrows; rare splash volumes that burn.
- Exits: four unique authored landmarks (navy bridge window, shuttle nozzles, barn door on plinth, lighthouse beacon), each ExitTrigger; placement seeded so some are unreachable from some starts.
- Verticality: piers 2-6 m above ground; tall dunes only as backdrop.
- ABSENT: bases, friendly NPCs, food, water, crossing the tar on foot, access to the dunes.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not let the player reach the dunes.
- Do not add allied camps or loot-rich islands (no edible materials).
- Do not make the tar walkable or swimmable as a route.
- Do not add Level 40/42, Macchina or Lost.
- Do not add more entity types.
- Do not make piers connect to islands directly.
