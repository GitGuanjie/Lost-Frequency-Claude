## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
- SOURCE FACT: 25 km2 central hill field in constant daylight inside a night forest -> hand-authored central hill terrain (heightfield to ~80 m, median 25 m) in bright daylight with dense fog, surrounded by chunk-streamed night forest ring. Grass material zoysia-green, moss, loam.
- SOURCE FACT: fog densest in valleys -> height-based fog volume with low visibility in valleys; no compass aid in-game (page stresses direction loss).
- SOURCE FACT: no Nguithr'xurh in the central area, concentrated within 1 km of border -> forest ring with sourced colonies (hatchery cluster props); entity threat only in forest; central area safe from them.
- SOURCE FACT: sheep -> ambient flock agents in the hills; no standing water, so no water volumes.
- Entrance: spawn in forest ~4 km from border. Exit: beyond 6 km into the forest, 65% roll per check (seeded) to noclip out.
- Murphy Estate: not located in the text (inner workings unknown) - do not build a homestead interior.
- Verticality: hills up to 80 m only.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not place Nguithr'xurh in the central area.
- Do not add standing water.
- Do not invent the Murphy Estate layout.
- Do not make the forest daytime.
- Do not add other exits beyond the forest noclip and B.N.T.G. route (unspecified).
