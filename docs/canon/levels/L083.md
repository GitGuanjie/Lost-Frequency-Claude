## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Sealed 600 m deep submarine → compact authored interior (berth, canteen, windowed corridors, reactor-adjacent compartments) of metal and plaster/panels; working lights; windows onto dark water.
- Infinite food/water → fridge interact restocks flags (no hunger pressure).
- Great Observer → scripted non-hostile event: hull taps, creaking, camera shake, sudden eye at a window, no damage.
- Arrival always berth or canteen (random choice by seed).
- Exit: escape hatch interact → Level 7; also a timed trigger outside after 10 minutes.
- Audio: ventilation hum, reactor drone, hull creaks, rhythmic tapping.
- Verticality: modest, within one vessel; hatch at the top.
- ABSENT: working controls/steering, hostile entities, exploration of the outside seabed.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not make the sub drivable; controls are non-functional.
- Do not make the Great Observer hostile or carnivorous.
- Do not model an explorable seabed; outside is not explored.
- Do not invent a crew leader or rank.
- Do not impose hunger scarcity; supplies are infinite (though the crew notes limited portions for 100 people).
