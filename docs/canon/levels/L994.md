## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: an empty, quiet beach with a tide that returns; the sea sounds wrong; leave before the tide comes.
- Layout grammar: one finite beach strip, sea to the east, mountains on other sides, an iron EXIST gate in the west mountain; nothing else. Pebbles are scattered; tidal flat area varies up to ~20 km2 (scale down).
- Materials/lighting/audio: sand, pebbles, cliff rock, clear water; wave audio that becomes layered with machinery, voices and prayers as you approach the water.
- Stateful mechanic: a tide clock with irregular ebb and flood, a safe distance from the sea (stated values: 15 m, 30 m, 40 m), fog rising when the tide nears; the sea's audio distortion grows with proximity.
- Exits: the gate; reachable only before the flood. If the tide arrives first, the player is lost (run end).
- Verticality: none (cliff at the north end is sand only).
- Entity policy: NO ENTITY PER SOURCE; the faces appear only as sound and seen under clear water, near the sea edge.
- ABSENT: buildings, tools, plants, animals, a base.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not build a base or construction site; the level resists it.
- Do not add a visible creature; the Description says none exist.
- Do not make the tide periodic; it is irregular.
- Do not treat Richee's audio as verified; its authenticity "cannot be guaranteed".
- Do not give a second exit; only the entry gate is named.
