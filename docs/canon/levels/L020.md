## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT → IMPLEMENTATION
- Two zones: Waiting halls (white concrete, brown carpet, grey tile ceiling, lockers/vending machines/Almond Water fountains) built as chunked procedural halls; Electric (black concrete, grey carpet, neon emissive strips) as a very gently ramping descent (slopes of imperceptible rise) with a temperature variable falling as depth grows.
- Windows with void and stars: authored window props with unlit star-field; a 10 m radius zone applying cold, headache vignette, paranoia whispers.
- Games: five interactive rooms behind doors (foam-ball throws, climbing wall with red/green holds, laser dodging, ball-into-hole under timer). Win spawns supplies; lose spawns the sourced consequence (Liquid Pain flood, Wrangler/teleport, Hounds).
- Orange ooze drip from ceiling, harmless per article; suppress damage.
- Exits: staff doors (to Level 2), window noclip (random 24/78), game exit doors with sign (seeded rare chance).
- Verticality: only the slow downward slope. Entities: sourced list only, few in Waiting halls, more in the Electric, never inside game rooms.
- Absent: the planet surface unless the endgame log is deliberately dramatised as a hallucination note.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not let entities enter game rooms.
- Do not present the planet/sky entity as confirmed article fact; it is one testimony.
- Do not make the Electric steep; the slope is unnoticeable.
- Do not drop the cold: window zones and depth drive it.
- Do not give Level 20 a fixed exit from games; the chance is "rare".
