## Signature mechanic and in-game implementation plan
SOURCE FACT: huge bar-gated brick/electrical maze with a safe, enormous fortress base at its centre, and entities that act as organised brigades.
- Layout grammar: authored anchors (a hall-sized Base Gamma atrium, one Assembly Line, one Generator Room, one Boiler Room, one Sanctum) joined by procedural brick corridors of varied width/height (crawl gaps, side-step gaps, open halls). Bars as non-collidable-through slabs placed across many branches; a rare gate slab with a lock-pick interaction.
- Materials/lighting/audio: brick, concrete floor, dim flickering fluorescent pool, faint ballast hum under electrical crackle and liquid rushing in pipes; reverb long in big halls.
- Stateful mechanic: paranoia hotspots in dark narrow sections (audio-only hallucinations, no capture); Sanctum as entity-free refuge.
- Exits: elevator (random L4/L5), Base Gamma door to L11 (interact), occasional car room to L69 (seeded probability).
- Verticality: Base Gamma has stacked sectors (Residence above Public, Administration above); keep to a few floors. No tall towers elsewhere.
- Entity policy: only the sourced entities; if implemented, a single coordinated pursuer pack, never a lone wanderer.
- ABSENT: Level 11 pacifying effect, lava, daylight, unlimited-fantasy loot beyond the Assembly Line.

## Canon traps (do not)
- Do not let bars be broken, noclipped or bypassed; the page states every attempt failed.
- Do not put statues of the angel deity in a movable inventory or let Sanctum images repel entities (only the physical rooms do).

- Do not treat the notebook author's claims (millennia, a prison) as article fact; they are in-universe documents.
- Do not make Empress Deathmoths or their hive present; they are wanderer claims.
